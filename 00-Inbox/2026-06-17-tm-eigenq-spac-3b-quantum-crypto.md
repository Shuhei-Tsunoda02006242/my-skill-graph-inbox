## Key Claim
EigenQ is merging with SPAC Silicon Valley Acquisition Corp. (SVAQ) at a $3B enterprise value to accelerate deployment of its post-quantum cryptography platform — the company builds encryption systems designed to remain secure against future quantum computer attacks.

## Evidence / Context
- SPAC partner: Silicon Valley Acquisition Corp. (NASDAQ: SVAQ), with ~$215M in trust prior to redemptions
- Combined company to list on Nasdaq as ticker "EIGQ"; expected to close Q4 2026, pending shareholder + regulatory approval
- Boards of both companies unanimously approved the merger
- Strategic partners include HPE, AMD, TD SYNNEX, and WNC — spanning enterprise compute, distribution, and networking
- Focus: post-quantum cryptography (PQC) — replacing current encryption algorithms (RSA, ECC) with quantum-resistant alternatives ahead of the "harvest now, decrypt later" threat window
- Company also develops quantum security, communications, and sensing technology
- NIST finalized its first PQC standards in 2024, creating a regulatory deadline for enterprise migration
