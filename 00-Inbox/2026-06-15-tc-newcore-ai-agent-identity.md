## Key Claim
NewCore emerged from stealth with $66M in seed funding (at a $300M valuation) to build an identity security platform that manages both human and AI-agent identities, treating AI agents as first-class identities with their own permissions, lifecycle controls, and revocation mechanisms.

## Evidence / Context
- Seed round led by Cyberstarts, with participation from Index Ventures and Evolution Equity Partners, plus angels including Assaf Rappaport (Wiz) and Yevgeny Dibrov (Armis).
- Tel Aviv/San Francisco-based, founded 2025 by Zohar Alon (co-founder of Dome9, acquired by Check Point for $175M), Amihai Neiderman (founder of Nym, ex-Unit 8200), and Erez Yarkoni (former CIO of T-Mobile and Telstra).
- Framing: as AI agents become "employees" inside organizations, existing identity/access management systems (built for humans) need to be rebuilt to authenticate, govern, and control agents at scale.
