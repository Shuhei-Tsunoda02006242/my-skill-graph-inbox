## Key Claim
Cognition, maker of autonomous AI software engineer Devin, raised $1B+ at a $25B pre-money valuation ($26B post), doubling from its $10.2B valuation just eight months earlier, as enterprise adoption of AI coding agents accelerates beyond IDE-copilot tools.

## Evidence / Context
- $1B+ round led by Lux Capital, General Catalyst, and 8VC; new investors include Ribbit Capital, Atreides, Layer Global
- Post-money valuation: $26B (up from $10.2B in September 2025 — 2.5× in 8 months)
- ARR: $492M annualized run-rate
- Enterprise customer growth: 50% month-over-month for six consecutive months
- Customers include Mercedes-Benz, NASA, Goldman Sachs, Santander
- Devin operates as an agent-first autonomous coder, not a copilot — handles full engineering workflows
- 90% of Cognition's own codebase is written by Devin
