## Key Claim
OpenAI is preparing a major ChatGPT overhaul that would turn it into an agent-driven "super app" bundling coding tools (Codex) and AI agents, aiming to win enterprise customers from Anthropic and move closer to profitability ahead of an IPO.

## Evidence / Context
- OpenAI has merged its ChatGPT and Codex product teams into a single unit to build a unified agentic platform
- Thibault Sottiaux, who leads OpenAI's core product and platform, says the goal is "your own personal agent that is capable of helping you ... across everything in your life, be it personally or at work"
- A senior OpenAI employee reportedly said "Chat is dead," signaling a shift away from the conversational interface as the core product
- Reuters previously reported OpenAI has drawn up confidential IPO filings, though Sam Altman insists there is "no deadline for an IPO"
- The strategy effectively uses free ChatGPT as a funnel toward paid products like Codex
