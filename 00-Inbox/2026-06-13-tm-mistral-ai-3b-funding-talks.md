## Key Claim
French AI lab Mistral AI is in early-stage talks to raise approximately €3 billion (~$3.5B) at a valuation of about €20 billion, nearly double its €11.7 billion valuation from a Series C round just nine months earlier.

## Evidence / Context
- Reported by Bloomberg on June 12, 2026; terms are still in flux and discussions are early-stage.
- Previous valuation was €11.7 billion, set during a September 2025 funding round.
- ASML Holding is Mistral's largest shareholder, having invested €1.3 billion for an 11% stake in the prior round.
- The new capital would fund Mistral's compute buildout as it competes with US and Chinese frontier AI labs.
