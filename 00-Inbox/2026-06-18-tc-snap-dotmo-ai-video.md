## Key Claim
Snap is spinning off its internal generative AI video team into an independent startup called Dotmo, citing the unsustainable cost of running frontier AI video model R&D inside a consumer social app — Snap retains a large equity stake in exchange.

## Evidence / Context
- Dotmo will focus on building AI models that generate interactive gaming experiences, not social media content.
- Snap receives a large equity stake in Dotmo, plus grants it a technology license to adapt Snap's underlying tech for gaming and interactive entertainment.
- This is Snap's second major spinoff of 2026 — it also spun off its Specs smart glasses team into a separate company earlier this year.
- High cost of generative video model R&D was cited as the primary driver; operating it internally was not commercially sustainable.
