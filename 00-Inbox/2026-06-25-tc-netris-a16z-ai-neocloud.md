## Key Claim
Netris raised $15M from Andreessen Horowitz to automate the networking layer inside GPU data centers via its NAAM (Network Automation, Abstraction, and Multi-Tenancy) platform, cutting deployment timelines for AI neoclouds that would otherwise require armies of network engineers.

## Evidence / Context
- $15M Series A led by a16z; Guido Appenzeller (former CTO VMware Cloud & Networking) joining the board
- 800% ARR growth in the past 12 months; 35+ live deployments across major neoclouds, sovereign AI operators, and AI factories
- Claims more live deployments than all other network-automation vendors combined
- Uses deterministic algorithms (not AI) — intentional design choice for reliability in critical infrastructure
- Expanding to Singapore; use of funds: engineering, commercial, ecosystem partnerships
- Addresses a real scaling bottleneck: spinning up large GPU clusters is gated on manual network configuration
