Probably raised a $9M seed round from Andreessen Horowitz to build infrastructure that catches AI hallucinations and factual errors before they reach users, targeting 99.99% accuracy — a reliability bar common in deterministic systems but elusive for LLMs.

## Evidence / Context
- $9M seed led by Andreessen Horowitz
- First product: a data science tool that produces quick, verified answers from complex datasets
- Hallucinations remain endemic to large language models even in the most capable frontier models
- Goal is a reliability layer that sits between LLM output and the end user, not a new model
