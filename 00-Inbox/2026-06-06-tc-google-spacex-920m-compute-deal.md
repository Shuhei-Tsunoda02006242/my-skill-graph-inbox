## Key Claim
Google agreed to pay SpaceX roughly $920 million per month (about $30 billion over the contract's life, October 2026 through June 2029) for access to ~110,000 Nvidia GPUs and related compute at xAI's data centers — a sign that AI compute scarcity is turning would-be rivals into each other's infrastructure landlords.

## Evidence / Context
- Deal disclosed in a regulatory filing on Friday, June 5; full $920M/month rate starts October 2026, with a reduced ramp-up fee through September
- Covers approximately 110,000 Nvidia GPUs plus CPUs, memory, and related components at xAI/SpaceX data centers (including the Colossus facility near Memphis)
- Google's stated rationale: "a short-term, timely agreement to ensure we have bridge capacity to meet surging customer demand for our agent platform, Gemini Enterprise, which has been even higher than we expected"
- Announced one week before SpaceX's expected Nasdaq debut (ticker SPCX), where the company is targeting roughly a $75B raise at a ~$1.75T valuation
- Mirrors a similar arrangement in which Anthropic agreed to pay xAI/SpaceX $1.25 billion per month through 2029 for all available compute from Colossus 1
- Both the Google and Anthropic deals include 90-day cancellation clauses exercisable after December 31, 2026
