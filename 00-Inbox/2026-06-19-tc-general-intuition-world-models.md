## Key Claim
General Intuition, which trains embodied AI and world models on roughly 2 billion gaming video clips per year generated by Medal's 10 million monthly active users, is in talks to raise $300M at a ~$2B valuation — just 8 months after its $134M seed round.

## Evidence / Context
- Spun out of Medal B.V. (gaming clip platform) in October 2025 with a $134M seed
- Medal dataset: ~2 billion clips/year across thousands of games; 10M+ monthly active users uploading gameplay footage
- Founded by Pim de Witte (Medal co-founder) and researchers Eloi Alonso, Adam Jelley, and Vincent Micheli — all known for diffusion-based simulation and world modeling research
- Investors in talks include Jeff Bezos, Eric Schmidt, Khosla Ventures (lead), and General Catalyst
- Core thesis: gaming video data is the richest available signal for training models to understand how objects move, interact, and obey physical laws in real time
- Total funding to date (if round closes): ~$434M
