## Key Claim
Barcelona-based Theker raised $85M in what it calls "Europe's largest-ever robotics Series A" to build factory robots that are reconfigurable rather than built around a fixed humanoid form, betting that generalist, swappable hardware beats single-purpose machines for industrial automation.

## Evidence / Context
- $85M Series A, roughly double the company's original fundraising target
- Round led by US VC firm CRV, with strategic backing from Samsung and Aglaé Ventures (LVMH chairman Bernard Arnault's investment vehicle)
- Unlike fixed-form humanoid robots (e.g., Boston Dynamics), Theker's machines have swappable arms, hands, and overall form depending on the task
- Headquartered in Barcelona, which the article frames as a growing European robotics hub
- Positions Theker among Europe's most-watched deep tech startups
