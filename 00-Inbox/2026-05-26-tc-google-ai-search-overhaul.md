## Key Claim
Google replaced its 25-year-old link-list search paradigm with AI-powered interactive experiences and autonomous information agents at Google I/O 2026 — the biggest structural change to Search since its inception.

## Evidence / Context
- New "intelligent search box" drops users into AI-powered interactive experiences instead of returning a list of links
- Autonomous "information agents" dispatched to gather data on a user's behalf
- Users can build personalized mini-apps tailored to their needs directly within Search
- Powered by Gemini 3.5 Flash (announced the same week), optimized for speed and agentic tasks
- Integrated with Gemini Spark (Google's 24/7 personal AI assistant inside Gmail)
- Launched globally as the new default at Google I/O 2026
- Clarification issued post-publication: links are not entirely removed, but significantly de-emphasized
