## Key Claim
Prometheus, the physical AI startup co-founded by Jeff Bezos and ex-Verily co-founder Vik Bajaj, raised $12B at a $41B valuation to build an "artificial general engineer" — AI software meant to automate the design and manufacturing of complex physical systems, from jet engines to drug compounds.

## Evidence / Context
- $12B raised at a $41B valuation — the second fundraise for Prometheus, following an initial $6.2B raise when it launched late last year.
- Backers include Bezos personally, plus JPMorgan Chase, Goldman Sachs, and BlackRock.
- The company employs about 150 people across San Francisco, London, and Zurich.
- Bezos frames the opportunity around "labor scarcity" — AI productivity gains for engineering work outpacing the supply of human engineers.
