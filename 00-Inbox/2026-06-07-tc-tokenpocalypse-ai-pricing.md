## Key Claim
Microsoft's switch of GitHub Copilot from flat-rate to token-based billing has triggered a developer "Tokenpocalypse" backlash, exposing how the broader AI industry's investor-subsidized pricing is starting to give way to costs being passed on to end users.

## Evidence / Context
- GitHub Copilot moved to usage-based token billing on June 1; developers report projected monthly costs jumping from $29 to roughly $750, and from $50 to $3,000 in heavy agentic-coding workflows
- Quote: "This whole ecosystem is heavily, heavily subsidized by investor money. And so stuff that seems like it has no cost is, in fact, incredibly expensive ... now we're going to get to a point where more of that cost is going to get passed on to the end consumer"
- Rising adoption of autonomous coding agents is driving total token consumption higher even as per-token prices fall
- The piece notes Anthropic's forthcoming IPO S-1 will likely need to disclose token-cost-related risk factors, with other AI vendors expected to follow Microsoft's lead on price hikes and usage caps
