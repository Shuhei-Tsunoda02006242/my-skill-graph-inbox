## Key Claim
Google's Gemini 3.5 Flash outperforms its own frontier model (3.1 Pro) on nearly all benchmarks and is 4x faster — representing a deliberate strategic pivot from conversational AI to autonomous agentic systems.

## Evidence / Context
- Gemini 3.5 Flash "outperforms 3.1 Pro on nearly all benchmarks" including coding, agentic tasks, and multimodal reasoning, per DeepMind chief technologist Koray Kavukcuoglu
- 4x faster than other frontier models; optimized for low-latency autonomous execution
- Model can independently execute coding pipelines, manage research projects, and in internal tests built an OS from scratch
- Announced as the default model in the Gemini app and Google Search's AI Mode globally
- Powers Gemini Spark, Google's new 24/7 personal AI agent with Gmail integration
- Available via Gemini API, Antigravity platform, and Gemini Enterprise
