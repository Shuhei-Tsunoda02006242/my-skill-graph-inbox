## Key Claim
onsemi (ON Semiconductor) is acquiring Synaptics in a $7 billion all-stock deal—its largest ever—to become a vertically integrated Physical AI chip company, adding Synaptics' edge compute, wireless connectivity, and human-machine interface portfolio to its existing power and sensing business.

## Evidence / Context
- Deal value: ~$7B all-stock; 1.350 onsemi shares per Synaptics share; 19% premium on 10-day VWAP
- Closing expected mid-2027, pending regulatory and shareholder approval
- $200M in synergies projected (85–90% from OpEx/SG&A)
- onsemi's TAM expands by $30B to reach $243B by 2030
- Synaptics brings: SynaOne edge-compute platform, wireless IoT connectivity portfolio, HMI hardware
- Physical AI thesis: four pillars = Power, Sense, Connected Compute, Control — enabling machines to sense, decide, act, and adapt in the physical world
- onsemi stock dropped ~20% on announcement due to all-stock dilution concerns
