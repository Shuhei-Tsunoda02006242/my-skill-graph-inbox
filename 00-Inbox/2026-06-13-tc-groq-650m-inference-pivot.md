## Key Claim
After Nvidia paid roughly $20B in December to license Groq's chip technology and hire away senior engineers without fully acquiring the company, Groq is now raising $650M from existing investors to rebuild around a narrower "inference neocloud" business built on its own chips.

## Evidence / Context
- December deal: Nvidia paid ~$20B in cash to Groq's investors (what would have been Nvidia's largest-ever purchase price), licensed Groq's chip technology, and took senior engineers -- without absorbing the company.
- New $650M round is being sought from the same investors who were cashed out in December, now asked to reinvest; Disruptive and Infinitium have agreed to fill the round if others decline their pro-rata shares.
- Groq's inference speed (tokens per second) has consistently benchmarked above Nvidia GPU-based inference at comparable price points.
- Result: a financially reset, technically depleted Groq rebuilding around inference-as-a-service.
