## Key Claim
Adaption's AutoScientist co-optimizes both training data and the model simultaneously, automating the fine-tuning process and reportedly more than doubling win rates across different base models — without human-in-the-loop dataset curation.

## Evidence / Context
- AutoScientist builds on Adaption's existing "Adaptive Data" product, which continuously improves datasets; the new layer turns those improving datasets into continuously improving models
- Co-founder and CEO Sara Hooker previously VP of AI Research at Cohere
- Adaption raised a $50M seed round led by Emergence Capital, with Mozilla Ventures, Fifty Years, and Threshold Ventures participating
- Company claims >2x win-rate improvement across different models; conventional benchmarks (SWE-Bench, ARC-AGI) not applicable since the system adapts to specific tasks
- AutoScientist available free for the first 30 days post-launch (launched May 13, 2026)
