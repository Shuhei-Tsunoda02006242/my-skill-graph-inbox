## Key Claim
Private fusion energy funding has surpassed $13 billion across at least 17 startups, with a wave of $100M+ rounds in H1 2026 signalling the sector is maturing from science experiment to capital-intensive infrastructure race.

## Evidence / Context
- Total private fusion funding: >$13B; 17 startups have each raised $100M+
- Commonwealth Fusion Systems: ~$3B raised; SPARC tokamak reactor ~75% complete in Devens, MA
- TAE Technologies: $1.79B; Helion Energy: $1.5B; Pacific Fusion & Shine Technologies: ~$1B each
- General Fusion: >$600M raised
- Focused Energy: $240M Series A (June 2026, laser-powered fusion)
- Thea Energy (Princeton spin-out): $100M Series B, led by U.S. Innovative Technology Fund (May 2026)
- UK entrants: Tokamak Energy ($336M), First Light Fusion ($108M)
- Macro tailwinds: more powerful compute chips, sophisticated AI, and high-temperature superconducting magnets are converging to make commercial fusion more tractable
