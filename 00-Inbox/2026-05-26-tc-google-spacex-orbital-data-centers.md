## Key Claim
Google and SpaceX are in early talks to co-develop AI data centers in orbit, with SpaceX pitching space as the future cheapest compute location — a narrative also supporting its upcoming $1.75T IPO.

## Evidence / Context
- SpaceX framing: orbital data centers will be cheaper than ground-based within a few years for AI workloads
- Cost reality today: 1-GW orbital data center estimated at ~$42.4B vs ~$14B on Earth — roughly 3× more expensive
- Google's Project Suncatcher: prototype satellites planned by 2027
- Anthropic already signed a deal with SpaceX to use Memphis xAI data center compute, with orbital future collaboration noted
- Technical hurdles: cosmic radiation corrupts compute, vacuum cooling works differently, added latency for real-time applications
- SpaceX IPO ($1.75T) narrative directly benefits from orbital compute story
