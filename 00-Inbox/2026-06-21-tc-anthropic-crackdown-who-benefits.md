## Key Claim
The Trump administration ordered Anthropic to immediately shut off global access to Claude Fable 5 and Claude Mythos 5, citing national security concerns — an outcome shaped less by a technical vulnerability than by the company's persistent refusals to cooperate with government surveillance and autonomous weapons programs.

## Evidence / Context
- Government directive arrived Friday at 5:21 p.m. ET; Anthropic was forced to disable both models worldwide, not only for targeted foreign nationals
- Amazon CEO Andy Jassy reportedly raised concerns with the White House after Amazon researchers found a way to bypass Fable 5's guardrails — framing the crackdown
- Root cause attributed to "personality differences": Anthropic previously refused to allow mass surveillance of Americans and fully autonomous weapons use
- In March, Trump administration already declared Anthropic a supply-chain risk, leading to an injunction Anthropic won
- Sales data from a June 16 TechCrunch report suggested the feud was paradoxically boosting Anthropic's enterprise sales in non-US markets
