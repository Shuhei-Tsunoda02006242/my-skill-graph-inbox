## Key Claim
Ambrosia Energy, founded by ex-SpaceX/Starlink engineers Sara Spangelo and Ben Longmier, emerged from stealth with a solar-plus-battery power plant design that delivers round-the-clock electricity at roughly $100/MWh — cheaper and faster to build than new natural gas plants — aimed at AI data center demand.

## Evidence / Context
- Cost target of ~$100/MWh versus ~$107/MWh for a new combined-cycle gas turbine.
- New gas turbines currently face a 5-7 year backlog; Ambrosia claims its plants can go from contract signing to power-on in 12 months, at any scale.
- The cost reduction comes from operational design — charging slowly during the day and discharging slowly at night — rather than new battery materials.
- Founders previously worked on Starlink-related projects at SpaceX.
