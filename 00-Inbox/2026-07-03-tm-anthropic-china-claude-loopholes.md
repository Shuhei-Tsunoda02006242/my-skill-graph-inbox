## Key Claim
Anthropic is closing loopholes that allowed Chinese companies including Ant Group and ByteDance to access its Claude models via overseas subsidiaries and cloud infrastructure (e.g. Microsoft Azure), in violation of Anthropic's terms of service.

## Evidence / Context
- Ant Financial gave staff corporate Claude accounts tied to a Singapore-based subsidiary; ByteDance reimbursed engineers for personal subscriptions purchased via VPN
- These workarounds do not violate US or Chinese law, but do breach Anthropic's ToS, which bars Chinese companies and foreign entities under their control
- Anthropic will now monitor account signals — including computer time zones and usage patterns — to detect accounts acting as "transfer stations" for China-linked firms
- CEO Dario Amodei said in February 2026 the company has "forgone several hundred million dollars in revenue" by cutting off CCP-affiliated firms and blocking CCP-sponsored cyberattacks
- The crackdown follows existing policy but tightens enforcement mechanics
- Original reporting: Financial Times (paywalled); confirmed across multiple secondary outlets on 2026-07-03
