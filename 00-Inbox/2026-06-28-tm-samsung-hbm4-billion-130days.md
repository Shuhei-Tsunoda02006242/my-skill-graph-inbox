## Key Claim
Samsung's HBM4 — the world's first mass-produced 6th-generation High Bandwidth Memory — surpassed $1 billion in cumulative revenue just 130 days after mass production began on February 12, 2026, with annual revenue projected to reach $10B by year-end.

## Evidence / Context
- Mass production start: February 12, 2026 (world's first HBM4)
- $1B revenue milestone: ~June 23, 2026 (130 days later, ~$972.5M at time of report)
- Projected annual run rate: ~$10B (~15.4 trillion KRW)
- Samsung's 2026 semiconductor capex: 110 trillion KRW (~$73B), a 128% increase vs. 2025's 47.5 trillion KRW — largest single-year semiconductor investment by any company in history
- Three strategic goals of the capex surge: scale HBM4 production, close technology/yield gap with TSMC in foundry, and build advanced packaging for AI chiplets
- Samsung chairman personally reviewed HBM4 supply chain upon hitting the $1B milestone
