## Key Claim
Cerebras Systems, maker of the world's largest AI chip (a full-wafer WSE), went public in May 2026 at a ~$60B valuation after nearly collapsing in 2019 while spending $8M/month to solve the wafer-scale manufacturing problem from scratch.

## Evidence / Context
- IPO priced at $185/share; closed first trading day at $311, up ~68%, briefly valuing the company near $67B
- Raised $4.8B in the IPO, making it 2026's largest tech offering
- In 2019, the company had burned nearly $200M trying to solve a single technical problem: how to cool and route data across a full-wafer chip without cracking it
- CEO Andrew Feldman: "We were spending about $8 million a month… we had incinerated nearly $200M trying to solve one technical problem"
- Team had to invent a custom machine that bolts 40 screws simultaneously to secure the wafer to a board
- Major customers include OpenAI (deal reportedly >$10B) and AWS
- Cerebras' chips are optimized for AI inference (serving), not training
