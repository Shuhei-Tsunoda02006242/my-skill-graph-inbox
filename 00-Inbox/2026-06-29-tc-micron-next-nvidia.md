## Key Claim
Wall Street is increasingly treating Micron as the AI infrastructure winner after it surpassed Meta and Tesla in market cap, driven by an acute shortage of High-Bandwidth Memory (HBM) — the chip that makes Nvidia GPUs viable at scale.

## Evidence / Context
- Micron Q3 2026 revenue: $41.5B, EPS $25 — both well above estimates; larger than Nvidia's net income at a comparable AI-growth stage
- Management confirmed all HBM production capacity is fully pre-sold through 2026
- The supply shortage has been dubbed "RAMageddon" and is expected to continue into 2027
- Micron briefly surpassed Meta and Tesla in market capitalisation
- AI data centers require HBM alongside Nvidia-class GPUs; Micron is one of only a handful of HBM suppliers globally
