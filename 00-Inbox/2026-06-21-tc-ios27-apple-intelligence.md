## Key Claim
Rather than centering iOS 27 AI on a redesigned Siri chatbot, Apple is distributing Apple Intelligence across existing apps — bill splitting from photos, smart message replies, on-device calendar parsing, and customer-service call assistance — all running locally for privacy.

## Evidence / Context
- Bill splitting: Apple Intelligence extracts items, quantities, tip, and total from a receipt photo
- Smart Messages: one-tap in-context suggestions (add to reminders, share relevant photos) powered by Apple Intelligence
- Customer service calls: surfaces needed info (e.g., account numbers) pulled from Mail, fully on-device
- Calendar: natural language event creation — Apple Intelligence extracts contacts, locations, and titles
- iOS 27 also allows users to choose from multiple AI models (announced May 2026: "Choose Your Own Adventure" model switching)
- Anthropic's Claude models currently blocked in US government context (separate story), creating uncertainty for any iOS 27 third-party model integration
