## Key Claim
Anthropic closed a $65B Series H at a $965B post-money valuation — almost certainly its final private round — making it the closest any startup has come to a $1 trillion private valuation and setting up what could be a landmark IPO.

## Evidence / Context
- Round co-led by Altimeter Capital, Dragoneer, Greenoaks, Sequoia Capital, Capital Group, Coatue, D1 Capital Partners
- Also includes Baillie Gifford, Blackstone, Brookfield, D.E. Shaw, DST Global, Fidelity, and strategic hardware partners Samsung, SK Hynix, Micron
- $15B of the round consists of previously committed hyperscaler investments, including $5B from Amazon (announced April 2026)
- Run-rate revenue crossed $47B earlier in May 2026
- WSJ reported 130% revenue surge expected, bringing Anthropic to its first operating profit
- Valuation trajectory in 2026: $350B (Jan) → $380B (Feb) → $965B (May)
