## Key Claim
Cerebras posted Q1 2026 GAAP revenue of $193.4M (core $191.3M, +92% YoY), its first earnings since a May 2026 IPO, and disclosed a multi-year $20B-plus deal for OpenAI to use 750MW of Cerebras ultra-low-latency compute capacity through 2028.

## Evidence / Context
- Q1 revenue $193.4M (+92% YoY vs. $99.5M in Q1 2025)
- OpenAI contract: 750MW ultra-low-latency AI inference, $20B+ value, capacity coming online in tranches through 2028
- Launched multi-year partnership with Amazon to bring Cerebras inference to AWS
- Cerebras raised $6.4B at IPO (largest semiconductor IPO of all time)
- Stock extended losses overnight despite the OpenAI deal — analyst flagged "concentration rotated, it didn't go away" (dependence on single large customer)
- Reported June 23–24, 2026 (CNBC, Reuters, SEC 8-K)
