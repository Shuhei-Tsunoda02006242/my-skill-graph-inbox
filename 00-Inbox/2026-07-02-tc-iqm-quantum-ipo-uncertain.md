## Key Claim
IQM became the first European quantum company to list on a major US exchange (Nasdaq, ticker IQMX), but shares spent most of their debut day below the IPO price after the company admitted in its own prospectus that "large-scale commercial traction of quantum computing technology may never occur."

## Evidence / Context
- Founded 2018 as a spinout from Aalto University (Espoo, Finland)
- Listed via SPAC merger with Real Asset Acquisition Corp at ~$1.9B valuation
- Dual-listed: Nasdaq (IQMX) and Nasdaq Helsinki
- Customer base grew from 8 (2024) to 22 (2025), primarily academic and research institutions
- Shares slipped below IPO price on first day of trading — lukewarm market reception
