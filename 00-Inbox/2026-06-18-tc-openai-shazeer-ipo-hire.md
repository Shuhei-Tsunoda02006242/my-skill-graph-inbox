## Key Claim
OpenAI has hired Noam Shazeer — co-inventor of the Transformer architecture and former Gemini co-lead at Google DeepMind — as its head of AI architecture research, a major technical coup immediately before the company's confidential IPO filing.

## Evidence / Context
- Shazeer co-authored the seminal 2017 paper "Attention Is All You Need," which introduced the Transformer architecture underpinning all modern LLMs.
- He previously co-founded AI startup Character AI; Google bought him back for roughly $2.7 billion before he left again to join OpenAI.
- At OpenAI, Shazeer will lead AI architecture research — studying how to design next-generation model architectures.
- OpenAI also hired Dean Ball (former Trump White House AI policy official) to lead a new "Strategic Futures" team starting July 6, 2026.
- OpenAI has confidentially filed for an IPO.
