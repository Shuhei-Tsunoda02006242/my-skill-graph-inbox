## Key Claim
OpenAI and Broadcom unveiled "Jalapeño," OpenAI's first custom LLM inference chip, developed from initial design to tape-out in approximately nine months — potentially the fastest ASIC development cycle ever for high-performance semiconductors.

## Evidence / Context
- Chip name: Jalapeño; purpose-built for LLM inference (not training)
- Engineering samples already running ML workloads in the lab at production target frequency and power, including GPT-5.3-Codex-Spark
- Early results show performance-per-watt "substantially better than current state-of-the-art"
- OpenAI's own AI models accelerated chip development
- Multi-generation roadmap: Jalapeño is the first step; targets initial production deployment by end of 2026
- Third partner Celestica handles board, rack, and system integration
- Strategic context: reduces OpenAI's dependence on Nvidia GPUs for inference
