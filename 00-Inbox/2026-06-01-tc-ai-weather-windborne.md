## Key Claim
WeatherMesh 6 is "as accurate five days out as a traditional forecast is the day before," beating the European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasting (ECMWF) on surface temperature and other key variables.

## Evidence / Context
- Produces a forecast every hour vs. every 6 hours for traditional models
- Resolution now down to 3 km in Europe and the continental US
- ~400 balloons in flight at any given time, launched from 15 global sites
- Founded 2019 by Stanford students; originally sold raw weather data, pivoted to building its own model when weather-forecasting deep learning models arrived in 2022
- ECMWF is the benchmark: the intergovernmental body seen by meteorologists as the world's leading weather-prediction provider
