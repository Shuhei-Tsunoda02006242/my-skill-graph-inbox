## Key Claim
ZeroDrift raised a $10M oversubscribed seed round to run a compliance firewall between AI models and enterprise end users, checking every AI-generated message, voice call, and video against regulations like SEC, FINRA, MiFID II, GDPR, and HIPAA in real time.

## Evidence / Context
- $10M seed round closed within 3 weeks, oversubscribed 3x; investors include a16z Speedrun and Reign Ventures
- Platform intercepts AI-generated output before it reaches users, flagging or replacing non-compliant content on the fly
- Early customers include tier-one banks, asset managers, and insurance companies
- Founder Kumesh Aroomoogan previously built Accern (raised $60M, acquired 2025)
- Targets regulated industries where AI chatbots and agents face strict communication compliance requirements
