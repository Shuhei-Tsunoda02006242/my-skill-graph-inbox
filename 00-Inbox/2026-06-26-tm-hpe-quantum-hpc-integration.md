## Key Claim
HPE announced expanded collaborations with eight quantum computing companies — spanning trapped-ion, superconducting, and photonic qubit approaches — to build hybrid quantum-HPC reference architectures and validate enterprise workflows, making HPE's supercomputer stack the integration layer for near-term quantum utility.

## Evidence / Context
- Eight partners: Intel, IQM, Qblox, Quantinuum, QuEra Computing, Quantum Machines, Rigetti, Riverlane
- Scope: joint hybrid reference architectures, validated application workflows, selected enterprise customer pilots
- Target use cases: high-value scientific and industrial applications combining quantum + classical HPC + AI
- Quantinuum-HPE bilateral deal announced June 22, 2026 — shortly after Quantinuum's Nasdaq IPO ($1.68B raised, ticker QNT, ~$15.7B market cap at debut)
- QNT stock jumped on HPE news
- HPE provides the large-scale HPC hardware layer; quantum partners supply QPU integration across diverse architectures
