## Key Claim
Micron's Q3 revenue quadrupled YoY to $41.45B with profit rising from $1.88B to $28.2B as AI data-center demand creates a sustained High-Bandwidth Memory shortage — dubbed "RAMageddon" — predicted to persist through 2027, leading Wall Street to draw explicit Nvidia comparisons.

## Evidence / Context
- Q3 revenue: $41.45B (4× YoY); profit: $28.2B (vs $1.88B prior year)
- Q4 guidance: $49–51B revenue
- HBM capacity for 2026 fully sold out under binding long-term contracts signed before the year began
- Multi-year supply deal + strategic equity investment in Anthropic's Series H
- Long-term contracts with Nvidia, Microsoft AWS, Google, Meta, Oracle
- Stock surged ~236% in a month; briefly surpassed Meta and Tesla market caps (~$1.2T)
- "RAMageddon" shortage (DRAM + NAND + HBM) driven by AI training and inference buildout; supply crunch predicted to last into 2027
