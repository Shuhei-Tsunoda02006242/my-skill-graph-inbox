## Key Claim
SpaceX filed plans for a "Terafab" — a multi-phase, vertically integrated semiconductor manufacturing and advanced computing fabrication facility in Grimes County, Texas — with an initial cost estimate of $55B and total expansion potential reaching $119B.

## Evidence / Context
- Initial phase estimated at $55B; total multi-phase buildout up to $119B (cost estimate grew from ~$20B in March 2026 to $119B by May)
- Located in Grimes County, Texas; public hearing scheduled June 3, 2026
- Target applications: AI servers, SpaceX satellites, proposed data center in space, Tesla autonomous vehicles and robots
- Intel signed on as a manufacturing partner alongside SpaceX and Tesla
- Would be a "next-generation, vertically integrated" fab — not just assembly but full chip manufacturing
- Grimes County Commissioners Court considering a tax abatement agreement to attract the project
