## Key Claim
Beijing is now requiring researchers, startup founders, and executives at private AI firms to seek government approval before traveling abroad, escalating its grip on AI talent as a strategic national asset.

## Evidence / Context
- Chinese authorities first advised top AI founders/researchers to avoid U.S. travel in March 2025 (Wall Street Journal)
- Restrictions have since expanded to private-sector firms, not just state-linked researchers
- China also requires government sign-off before companies like Moonshot AI, StepFun, and ByteDance can accept U.S. capital (Bloomberg, April 2026)
- Reflects Beijing treating AI talent as both an economic asset and national security priority
