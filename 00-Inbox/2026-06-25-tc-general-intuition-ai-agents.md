## Key Claim
General Intuition has raised $320M at a $2.3B valuation to train AI agents — and real-world robots — using billions of video game clips sourced from Medal, demonstrating that the same AI brain controlling a game agent can directly power a robot.

## Evidence / Context
- $320M Series A led by Khosla Ventures; total funding now $454M (after $134M seed in Oct 2025)
- Agent ran video games for 100+ continuous hours; same model architecture deployed to robotics
- Training data sourced from Medal (a gaming clip-sharing platform), providing large-scale, diverse action sequences
- Thesis: video games offer cheap, varied, physics-grounded environments for teaching spatial reasoning and sequential decision-making
