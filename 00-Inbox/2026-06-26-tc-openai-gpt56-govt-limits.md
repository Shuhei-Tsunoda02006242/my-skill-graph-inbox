## Key Claim
The Trump administration has restricted OpenAI's GPT-5.6 model family — comprising flagship Sol, balanced Terra, and fast/cheap Luna — to a "small group of trusted partners," approving access customer-by-customer; OpenAI publicly objects but complied, calling it a short-term step.

## Evidence / Context
- GPT-5.6 lineup: Sol (flagship), Terra (everyday balanced), Luna (fast, lower-cost) — all three restricted, not just the most capable model
- Government is "approving access customer by customer" during preview period per CEO Sam Altman's staff briefing
- OpenAI statement: "We don't believe this kind of government access process should become the long-term default. It keeps the best tools from users, developers, enterprises, cyber defenders, and global partners who need them."
- Framework being developed through a new executive order on cybersecurity
- Context: the Anthropic government ban on Fable/Mythos preceded this — the pattern is becoming structural, not one-off
- OpenAI framed GPT-5.6 preview as a "short-term step" toward broader availability in coming weeks
