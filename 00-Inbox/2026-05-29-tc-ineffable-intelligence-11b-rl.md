## Key Claim
Ineffable Intelligence, founded by ex-DeepMind RL lead David Silver, raised a $1.1B seed round (largest in European history) at a $5.1B valuation to build a "superlearner" AI that acquires knowledge entirely through trial-and-error reinforcement learning, without human-generated training data.

## Evidence / Context
- $1.1B seed, valuation $5.1B — largest European seed round ever recorded
- Co-led by Sequoia and Lightspeed; backed by Nvidia, Google, DST Global, Index Ventures, and the UK Sovereign AI Fund
- David Silver: former DeepMind RL research lead; created AlphaGo and AlphaZero — both learned game mastery purely from self-play
- Thesis: existing LLMs hit a data ceiling; RL-from-scratch bypasses human data bottleneck
- No product, no revenue, no public roadmap as of April 2026
- First model benchmarks expected by late 2026
