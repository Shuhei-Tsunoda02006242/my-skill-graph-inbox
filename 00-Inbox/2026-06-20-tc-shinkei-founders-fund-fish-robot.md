## Key Claim
Shinkei, backed by Founders Fund, deploys its Poseidon robot (computer vision + AI) to automate ike jime fish processing at scale — distributing the hardware free to fishermen and capturing margin through vertically integrated, premium distribution from catch to plate in Tacoma, WA.

## Evidence / Context
- Poseidon robot uses computer vision to scan each fish, identify species, locate the brain, and pierce it within seconds of leaving the water — an automated, industrial-scale version of the Japanese ike jime technique
- Slow death via thrashing/suffocation floods fish meat with stress hormones and lactic acid, degrading flavor and shelf life; ike jime eliminates this
- Shinkei provides Poseidon units to fishermen at no cost and pays above-dock-auction prices, taking full possession of the catch in exchange
- Business model is vertically integrated food processing, not hardware sales; unit economics depend on premium pricing downstream
- Founders Fund bet: re-shoring the full chain (catch → kill → process → distribute) under one roof can be done profitably enough to outcompete incumbents
