## Key Claim
Runway is pivoting from AI video generation into world models — AI systems that simulate and predict physical reality — positioning itself as a foundation model competitor to Google DeepMind, with $5.3B valuation and $40M ARR added in Q2 2026 alone.

## Evidence / Context
- Raised $315M Series E in February 2026 at $5.3B valuation, led by General Atlantic; investors include Nvidia, Adobe Ventures, AMD Ventures, AllianceBernstein, Fidelity
- Total raised: $860M since 2018 founding in New York
- Added $40M in ARR in Q2 2026
- Launched first world model in December 2025; second world model planned for 2026
- Strategic thesis: world models (not just video generation) will underpin the next wave of AI — from physical simulation to interactive synthetic environments
- Expanding beyond filmmakers into enterprises and new industries
