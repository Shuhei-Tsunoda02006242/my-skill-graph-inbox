## Key Claim
Nvidia challenger Etched has secured $1B+ in contracted orders for its transformer-dedicated inference chip (Sohu), with $800M raised total and a $5B valuation — backed by Jane Street and a TSMC-linked VC — as TSMC begins manufacturing the first silicon.

## Evidence / Context
- Co-founders Gavin Uberti (CEO) and Robert Wachen (president): Harvard dropouts, Thiel fellows
- Chip name: Sohu; an ASIC purpose-built for transformer model inference
- $800M raised total; most recent round: $500M at $5B valuation
- $1B+ in customer contracts already booked; chip currently in customer testing
- Investors include Jane Street and a TSMC-affiliated venture firm — strategic signal from supply-chain side
- Product: "frontier inference clusters" — chips + custom racks + software optimized for speed, cost, and power efficiency
- Roadmap: Taiwan factory operational; gigawatt-scale compute targeted for 2027
