## Key Claim
Groq officially confirmed a $650M round led by Disruptive and Infinitum, pivoting from chip hardware to AI inference neocloud after Nvidia paid ~$20B to license its technology and hired away founder Jonathan Ross and president Sunny Madra in late 2025.

## Evidence / Context
- Round led by Disruptive (Dallas late-stage VC, chairman Alex Davis) and Infinitum (Fort Lauderdale hedge fund)
- Last valued at $6.9B after September $750M round; new valuation not disclosed
- Neocloud: 13 data centers across North America, Europe, Middle East, and APAC; 5M+ developers; thousands of AI companies; trillions of tokens processed weekly
- New hires: Alan Rice (COO, ex-xAI and Meta), Sinclair Schuller (CTO), Rakesh Malhotra (CPO)
- Nvidia's December 2025 deal: non-exclusive license for Groq tech + hired founder and president; Nvidia shipped Groq-3 LPX inference system March 2026
- Groq's LPU (Language Processing Unit) architecture enables ultra-fast inference speeds
