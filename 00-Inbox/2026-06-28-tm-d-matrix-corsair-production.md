## Key Claim
d-Matrix's Corsair AI inference accelerator entered full production on June 9, 2026, delivering over 10x inference speed improvement versus GPU-only setups, with shipments now going to hyperscalers, neoclouds, and frontier AI labs.

## Evidence / Context
- Independent benchmark by Gimlet Labs: baseline 24-second response time cut to under 2 seconds when Corsair accelerators are paired with GPUs (vs. GPUs alone)
- Architecture: SRAM-based in-memory compute chiplets on organic substrate with LP-DDR5 memory — avoids expensive HBM-based CoWoS packaging used by Nvidia
- Demand driver: surge in agentic AI workloads (led by late-2025 breakouts like Claude Code) pushing inference beyond what GPU-only infrastructure was designed for
- Microsoft is a named backer; high-profile hyperscalers and frontier AI labs have made commitments
- Product: d-Matrix Corsair won 2026 AI Breakthrough Award
