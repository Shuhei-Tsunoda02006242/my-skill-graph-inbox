## Key Claim
Atom Computing (US) and Nu Quantum (UK) signed an MOU on June 17, 2026 to interconnect neutral-atom quantum processors using photonic networking hardware, targeting the GigaQuOp (one billion quantum operations) threshold — a milestone believed sufficient for fault-tolerant practical advantage.

## Evidence / Context
- The collaboration integrates Nu Quantum's photonic network switches and qubit-photon entanglement technology with Atom Computing's neutral-atom QPUs (AC1000 system: 1,200+ fully connected qubits)
- Goal: bypass physical space and control constraints of monolithic QPUs by clustering multiple discrete neutral-atom systems into high-performance distributed architectures
- Atom Computing also partners with Cisco to advance scalable networked quantum computing (complementary infrastructure angle)
- Atom Computing is one of ~9 companies receiving US government equity-for-investment support; raised $100M Series C on June 16 (Third Point Ventures, DCVC, Cisco Investments)
- Nu Quantum is a Cambridge University spin-out specialising in single-photon generation and quantum networking hardware
- The transatlantic MOU is non-binding but signals engineering collaboration with defined technical milestones
