## Key Claim
After Nvidia's $20B deal stripped out Groq's accelerator IP and senior leadership, Groq's remaining entity is raising $650M from existing backers to double down on its AI inference neocloud business.

## Evidence / Context
- Nvidia's December agreement licensed Groq's hardware accelerator architecture for ~$20B and absorbed much of its leadership
- Groq is now led by interim CEO Adam Winter and CFO Matt Eng
- $650M round targeted from existing investors Disruptive and Infinitum, offered pro rata to current shareholders via a "Groq2" successor vehicle
- Investors prepared to cover the full $650M if needed
- Business pivot focuses on the inference neocloud: renting AI processing power to developers and enterprises for model serving (not training)
- Groq retains its neocloud platform and existing customer contracts from the Nvidia deal
