## Key Claim
Amazon-owned Zoox revealed a refreshed robotaxi design based on real rider feedback and is targeting paid commercial rides in Las Vegas by late June 2026, pending a federal commercial exemption from NHTSA.

## Evidence / Context
- Vehicle specs: bidirectional driving, four-wheel steering, 40 cameras/radars/lidars/infrared sensors, up to 75 mph, 4 passengers
- Currently offering free rides in: Austin, San Francisco, Las Vegas, Miami
- Awaiting NHTSA commercial exemption (the vehicle has no standard driver controls mandated by federal law; an operational exemption was granted August 2025)
- Paid rides expected to launch Las Vegas by late June 2026 if approval granted
- Four-city paid expansion planned for 2026
- Competing in a heating robotaxi market (Waymo, Baidu, Tesla FSD)
