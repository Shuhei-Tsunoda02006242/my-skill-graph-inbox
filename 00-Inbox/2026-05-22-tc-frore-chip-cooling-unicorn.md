## Key Claim
Frore Systems reached unicorn status at a $1.64B valuation with a $143M Series D, positioning its liquid-cooling technology (LiquidJet, LiquidJet Nexus, AirJet) as foundational thermal infrastructure for AI chip deployments in data centers and edge computing.

## Evidence / Context
- $143M Series D led by MVP Ventures; $340M total raised
- Valuation: $1.64B
- Founded by two former Qualcomm engineers; initially built air-cooling for fanless consumer electronics
- Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang personally demo'd the tech and suggested pivoting to liquid cooling for AI chips — company executed on that suggestion
- Products target Nvidia GPU boards across data center and edge markets
- Investors include Fidelity, Qualcomm Ventures, Mayfield Fund, Clear Ventures, Addition, StepStone Group
- "Thermal stack" framing: Frore positioning heat management as a distinct, fundable infrastructure layer for the AI era
- Published March 16, 2026
