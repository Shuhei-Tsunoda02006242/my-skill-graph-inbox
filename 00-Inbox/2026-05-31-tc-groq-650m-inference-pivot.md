## Key Claim
After a $20B deal in December 2025 where Nvidia licensed Groq's chip technology and absorbed its senior leadership, the remaining Groq entity is raising $650M from existing backers to double down on its AI inference neocloud business.

## Evidence / Context
- Groq's deal with Nvidia (Dec 2025) was a "not-acqui-hire": ~$20B for IP licensing + departure of founder Jonathan Ross and other senior leaders to Nvidia
- Now raising $650M from existing investors; backers Disruptive and Infinitium guaranteed the round's fill
- Led by interim CEO Adam Winter and CFO Matt Eng
- Strategic pivot: from chip design to inference-as-a-service cloud using its proprietary LPU hardware
- Groq's LPU (Language Processing Unit) architecture offers significantly faster inference throughput than GPU-based alternatives
