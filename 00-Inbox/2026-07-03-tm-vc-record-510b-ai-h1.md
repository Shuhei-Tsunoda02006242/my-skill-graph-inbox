## Key Claim
Global venture funding reached a record $510B in H1 2026 — already ahead of all of 2025 ($440B) — but the headline is dominated by a handful of AI mega-rounds: OpenAI and Anthropic alone accounted for $217B, or 43% of total startup investment.

## Evidence / Context
- OpenAI raised $122B in Q1 2026 — larger than all prior quarterly totals ever recorded
- Anthropic raised $30B in Q1 and $65B in Q2 2026; near Q2 close Anthropic neared a $1T valuation
- xAI and Waymo were the other two mega-round drivers inflating the headline figure
- Crunchbase analysis: activity outside these four deals tracked near 2024-25 levels — making the record "fragile" as a signal of broad startup health
- In Q2 alone, VCs put $205B into 5,000+ startups
- Source: Gené Teare / Crunchbase News, surfaced on Techmeme 2026-07-02
