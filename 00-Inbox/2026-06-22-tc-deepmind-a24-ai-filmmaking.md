## Key Claim
Google DeepMind invested $75M in indie film studio A24 to co-develop AI filmmaking tools, explicitly framing the partnership around making films better rather than cheaper — a deliberate contrast to how most AI developers pitch Hollywood.

## Evidence / Context
- Investment: $75M from Google DeepMind into A24
- Structure: "First-of-its-kind" research partnership; DeepMind researchers embedded with A24 filmmakers
- Notable constraint: Google does NOT receive access to A24's content library or training data
- First project: AI-generated storyboard tools
- A24 tech lead: Scott Belsky (partner overseeing A24's technology division)
- A24 known for: "Everything Everywhere All At Once," "Marty Supreme," "Backrooms"
- Differentiation: Most AI-Hollywood pitches focus on cost/speed reduction; this deal focuses on quality and artist guidance feeding back into DeepMind R&D
