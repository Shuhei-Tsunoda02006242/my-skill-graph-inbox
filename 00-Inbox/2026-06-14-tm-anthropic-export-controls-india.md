## Key Claim
A US government directive forced Anthropic to suspend foreign nationals' access to its newest Fable 5 and Mythos 5 models worldwide, reigniting debate in India — Anthropic's second-largest market — over the country's dependence on US-controlled AI technology.

## Evidence / Context
- Suspension cites national security/cybersecurity concerns and applies to all foreign nationals globally, including Anthropic's own foreign employees
- Came one day after Anthropic and Tata Consultancy Services announced a partnership to train 50,000 employees on Claude and build a dedicated Anthropic business unit
- India's Claude run-rate revenue had doubled since October 2025; some Indian entities had just gained early access to Mythos via the "Project Glasswing" cybersecurity initiative
- Zoho founder Sridhar Vembu and Aarin Capital chairman Mohandas Pai called it a "wake-up call"; Pai proposed ~Rs 50,000 crore (~$6B) for India's national AI mission
- Marks a notable expansion of US export controls beyond hardware (chips) into AI software/models themselves
