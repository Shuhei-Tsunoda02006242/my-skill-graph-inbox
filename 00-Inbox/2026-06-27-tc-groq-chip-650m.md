## Key Claim
Groq confirmed a $650M fundraise and new executive hires after Nvidia's $20B licensing deal stripped away founder Jonathan Ross and senior staff, signaling a strategic pivot from custom AI hardware to AI inference cloud services.

## Evidence / Context
- $650M round led by Disruptive (Dallas, founded by Groq chairman Alex Davis) and Infinitum (Fort Lauderdale hedge fund)
- Nvidia signed a non-exclusive license for Groq's chip technology and hired founder/CEO Jonathan Ross for a reported $20B deal
- Groq's neocloud: 13 data centers across North America, Europe, Middle East, and APAC
- Serving 5M+ developers and thousands of AI companies, processing trillions of tokens/week
- New hires: Alan Rice as COO (ex-xAI, Meta), Sinclair Schuller as CTO, Rakesh Malhotra as CPO
