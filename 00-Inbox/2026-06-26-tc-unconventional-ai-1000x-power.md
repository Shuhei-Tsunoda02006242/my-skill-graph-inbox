## Key Claim
Unconventional AI, led by former Databricks AI chief Naveen Rao, released Un-0 — an image-generation model running on a software simulation of oscillator-based chips — claiming the architecture will ultimately reduce AI inference power consumption by 1,000x compared to conventional GPU-based systems.

## Evidence / Context
- $475M seed round raised at a $4.5B valuation, led by Andreessen Horowitz and Lightspeed Ventures (with Lux Capital, DCVC)
- Un-0 is the company's first public model; currently runs on a *software simulation* of Unconventional's oscillator chips — physical chip schematics to be released soon
- Oscillator-based architecture operates on fundamentally different principles than the transistor-based chips powering conventional LLMs
- Rao's prior role: head of AI at Databricks, which he left to found this company
- Inference (not training) is the target — the ongoing cost of running deployed AI models, not building them
- "The advantages of the oscillator-based computing are complex, but Rao believes it will ultimately reduce power use by as much as 1,000 times"
