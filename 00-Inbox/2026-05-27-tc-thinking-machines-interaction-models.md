## Key Claim
Thinking Machines Lab (founded by former OpenAI CTO Mira Murati) unveiled "interaction models" — a full-duplex architecture where the AI listens and speaks simultaneously, enabling natural interruption and sub-0.5s response times, unlike current turn-based AI voice systems.

## Evidence / Context
- Model name: TML-Interaction-Small; response latency 0.40 seconds (roughly natural human conversation speed)
- Claimed faster than comparable models from OpenAI and Google
- Architecture: single network with listening, speaking, visual perception, and pause detection trained in natively — not bolted on via pipeline
- Enables: simultaneous listen/speak, interruption recognition, visual cue response without explicit user commands
- Company raised $2B; limited research preview expected within months, wider release later in 2026
- Not yet publicly available
