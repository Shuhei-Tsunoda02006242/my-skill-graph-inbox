## Key Claim
IBM committed more than $10 billion over the next five years to quantum computing R&D, manufacturing, and M&A — including a $1 billion cash contribution to Anderon, which it calls the world's first pure-play quantum wafer foundry — with a clear roadmap to fault-tolerant quantum computing by 2029.

## Evidence / Context
- $10B+ investment spanning R&D, capex, manufacturing scaling, ecosystem partnerships, and acquisitions.
- **IBM Quantum Starling (2029):** First large-scale fault-tolerant quantum computer; projected to execute 20,000x more operations than today's systems.
- **IBM Quantum Blue Jay (post-2029):** Targets 1 billion quantum operations across 2,000 qubits.
- **Anderon foundry:** World's first pure-play quantum wafer foundry, formed with US Department of Commerce support. IBM contributing $1B cash plus significant IP, physical assets, and workforce.
- IBM is confident that partners using its systems will demonstrate quantum advantage in 2026.
- The investment covers the full stack: qubit hardware, error correction, compiler tooling, and application-layer partnerships.
