## Key Claim
Helion raised $465M in a Series G led by Thrive Capital, valuing it at $15.5B, as it races to complete its Orion fusion power plant and begin delivering electricity to Microsoft's grid as early as 2028.

## Evidence / Context
- Total capital raised now stands at $1.5B; prior round was $425M in January 2025
- Series G investors include Alta Park Capital, Anti Fund, BoxGroup, Lux Capital, Peak XV Partners, and Ford Executive Chairman Bill Ford
- Helion's approach: use magnets to compress fusion fuel and harvest electricity directly from the magnets (similar to regenerative braking in EVs)
- Existing power purchase agreement with Microsoft; separately in talks with OpenAI (March 2026)
- Targeting fusion power delivery to grid by 2028 — a contractual commitment, not just a roadmap
