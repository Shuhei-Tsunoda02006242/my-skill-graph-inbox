## Key Claim
Illinois is converting the former US Steel South Works site into the Illinois Quantum and Microelectronics Park — a $500M state-backed campus anchored by PsiQuantum, aiming to host the country's first utility-scale, error-corrected quantum computer. The economic impact on the Chicago region is projected to exceed $20 billion.

## Evidence / Context
- Campus broke ground in October 2025 with PsiQuantum's facility construction
- Tenants include IBM, Diraq, and Infleqtion alongside anchor PsiQuantum
- Illinois state invested $500M; projected >$20B regional economic impact and thousands of jobs
- Chicago largely missed the digital revolution; quantum is an explicit second-chance bet
- Reported by Jeanne Whalen / Wall Street Journal; Techmeme item 260624/p13
