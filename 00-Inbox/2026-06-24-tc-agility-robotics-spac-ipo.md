## Key Claim
Agility Robotics — maker of the Digit humanoid robot, already deployed commercially at Schaeffler, GXO, and Toyota manufacturing sites — is merging with Churchill Capital Corp XI (SPAC) at a $2.5B valuation to become a public company under ticker AGLT.

## Evidence / Context
- Valuation: $2.5B via SPAC merger with Churchill Capital Corp XI
- Gross proceeds expected to exceed $620M (Churchill XI holds ~$420M in trust; Foxconn leads PIPE)
- Existing backers: Amazon, Nvidia, SoftBank Vision Fund 2, Foxconn, DCVC
- Product: Digit v5 — described as "world's first AI-enabled cooperatively safe humanoid robot"
- Already working in live commercial environments with Schaeffler, GXO, Toyota Motor Manufacturing Canada
- Founded 2015 as spinout from Oregon State University
- Deal pending shareholder approval and SEC review
