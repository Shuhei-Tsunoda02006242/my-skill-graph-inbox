## Key Claim
SpinQ (Shenzhen) closed a RMB 1 billion (~$147M) Series D round on June 30, bringing its total funding over the past six months to RMB 2 billion, to build full-stack fault-tolerant general-purpose quantum computers.

## Evidence / Context
- Investors include CICC Capital, Shenzhen TopoScend Capital, Shanghai Semiconductor Industry Investment, AVIC Honghua, and Shanghai SEARI Private Equity — a mix of state-backed and strategic industry capital.
- SpinQ is targeting validation of d=3 surface-code quantum error correction (QEC) in 2026 and has published joint QEC research with HKUST accepted at QCE 2026.
- Hardware milestones: completed tape-out, packaging, and validation of new-generation 25-qubit and 103-qubit superconducting chips.
- Global reach: products and services now cover 40+ countries and 200+ universities, research institutions, and industry partners.
- Total funding raise pace (~$300M in 6 months) puts SpinQ among the best-funded quantum hardware companies globally.
