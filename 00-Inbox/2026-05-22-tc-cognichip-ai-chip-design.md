## Key Claim
Cognichip is building a physics-informed deep learning model to act as an AI engineering collaborator in chip design, claiming it can cut development costs by 75%+ and timelines by more than half — compressing a multi-year process.

## Evidence / Context
- $60M Series A led by Seligman Ventures; Intel CEO Lip-Bu Tan joined the board
- $93M total raised since founding in 2024
- Chip design currently takes 3–5 years end-to-end; design phase alone can be 2 years before physical layout begins
- Working with 30+ semiconductor design companies including major industry players
- Differentiator: embeds physics directly into the foundational model, enabling tradeoff calculations that pure data-driven EDA tools cannot do
- No chip has yet been fully designed using Cognichip's system (still pre-production validation)
- Published April 1, 2026
