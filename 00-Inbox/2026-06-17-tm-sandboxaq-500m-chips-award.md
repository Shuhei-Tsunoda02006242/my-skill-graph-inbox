## Key Claim
SandboxAQ secured a $500M CHIPS Act R&D award to use AI simulation and Large Quantitative Models (LQMs) to discover alternative semiconductor manufacturing materials, directly targeting US dependence on Chinese-controlled supply chains for rare-earth magnets and process chemicals.

## Evidence / Context
- China controls >90% of global neodymium-based permanent magnet production used in semiconductor equipment
- Four program focus areas: PFAS-free process chemicals, catalysts, rare earth-free magnets, battery systems
- SandboxAQ deploys its ReAQT simulation platform + LQMs — AI systems trained on laws of physics/chemistry rather than language — to virtually screen millions of candidate materials
- AI compression: what previously took decades of lab trial-and-error can now run as targeted discovery campaigns in weeks
- Unprecedented deal structure: US Department of Commerce receives a minority non-voting equity stake in SandboxAQ plus future royalty payments from formulas licensed to industrial partners
- SandboxAQ is an Alphabet spin-off focused on AI + quantum technology
