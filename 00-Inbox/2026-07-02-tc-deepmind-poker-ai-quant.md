## Key Claim
Three former DeepMind researchers who built DeepStack (the first AI to beat professional poker players) have pivoted to quantitative trading with Prague-based EquiLibre Technologies, raising a Series A at a $500M valuation with a claimed "perfect record of zero negative months since inception."

## Evidence / Context
- Team: CEO Martin Schmid, CTO Rudolf Kadlec, CSO Matej Moravcik — met as visiting PhD students at DeepMind's Edmonton office
- Built DeepStack, the first AI to beat pros at no-limit Texas hold'em (reinforcement learning)
- $10M seed led by Blossom Capital at $140M valuation → Series A led by Creandum at $500M valuation
- Creandum confirmed it was the "largest single investment the firm has ever made in one go"
- Partnership with quant firm Tower Research Capital; trading billions in daily volume across S&P 500, Nasdaq, and crypto
- Agents have posted zero negative months since inception (live since 2025 on crypto, then stocks)
