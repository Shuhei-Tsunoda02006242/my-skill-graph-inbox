## Key Claim
A new AI-generated benchmarking index (Road to Autonomy Index) ranks Baidu Apollo Go first globally in robotaxi deployment, with three of the top five slots held by Chinese companies — underscoring a structural advantage in scale, regulation, and manufacturing integration.

## Evidence / Context
- Autnmy AI built the Road to Autonomy Index using generative AI to parse federal/state reports, SEC filings, and public databases
- Ranking: 1. Baidu Apollo Go, 2. Waymo, 3. Pony.ai, 4. WeRide, 5. Tesla
- Index weighs operations, scale, revenue, commercial partnerships, manufacturing, and safety record
- Four separate indices: robotaxis, autonomous driving licensees, autonomous trucks, delivery bots
- China's Pony.ai previously announced plans to triple its global robotaxi fleet by end of 2026
- Waymo's newest robotaxi is Chinese-made (reported May 2026), reflecting hardware supply chain entanglement
