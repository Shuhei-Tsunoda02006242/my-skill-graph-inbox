## Key Claim
DeepSeek, the Chinese AI lab that shocked the world with its cost-efficient LLMs, is raising its first-ever VC round and its implied valuation has surged from $20B to $45B in just weeks, led by the Chinese state investment vehicle China Integrated Circuit Industry Investment Fund.

## Evidence / Context
- Founder Liang Wenfeng controls ~90% of the company; this is the first time outside investors are being brought in
- Primary motivation: retaining researchers against poaching by competitors — equity is the incentive
- Tencent and Alibaba reportedly in talks to participate alongside the state fund
- DeepSeek's models remain open-weight and competitive with top US models on reasoning and coding benchmarks
- Trained at a fraction of the compute cost of OpenAI/Anthropic equivalents — efficiency advantage is the core moat
- Valuation jumped from ~$20B to $45B within weeks of talks becoming public
