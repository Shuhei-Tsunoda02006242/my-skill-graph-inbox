## Key Claim
With the U.S. government pulling Anthropic's Fable/Mythos models and restricting GPT-5.6 to a pre-approved partner list, OpenAI and Anthropic now share the same existential challenge — government-controlled access to frontier models — making their head-to-head rivalry secondary to a sector-wide regulatory inflection point.

## Evidence / Context
- Anthropic's Fable and Mythos models were previously pulled/banned by the government
- OpenAI's GPT-5.6 (Sol, Terra, Luna) now entering "customer by customer" government-approval preview
- The article's argument: "OpenAI and Anthropic are now in the same exact position with the same problems facing them and the same disaster waiting if they fail"
- "The cost of implementing a haphazard government approval process for every frontier model is obvious, and there's no fix that helps one lab without helping the others"
- A new executive order on cybersecurity is being developed to create a more systematic framework
- Pattern suggests this could extend to other frontier labs (Google DeepMind, xAI) as models grow more capable
