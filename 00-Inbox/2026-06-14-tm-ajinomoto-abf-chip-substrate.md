## Key Claim
Ajinomoto, which controls over 95% of the global market for ABF (Ajinomoto Build-up Film) — a critical material for advanced AI chip packaging substrates — says it can meet demand through 2030 and plans to expand production capacity rather than raise prices.

## Evidence / Context
- ABF is essential for packaging substrates used in advanced AI GPUs and CPUs
- Ajinomoto's Functional Materials segment (centered on ABF) generated ¥54.6B in operating profit, up 35% year-over-year, ~30% of total operating profit, with margins exceeding 50%
- Company plans to invest at least ¥25 billion by 2030 to expand ABF capacity by 50% at its Gunma and Kawasaki plants, with a new plant targeted for 2032
- Surging AI chip demand is driving sharply higher ABF layer requirements per substrate, even as other reports flag a supply-demand gap persisting toward 2027-2028
