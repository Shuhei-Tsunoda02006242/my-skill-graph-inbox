## Key Claim
QuiX Quantum released Dedalo, a photonic quantum computing architecture roadmap designed to deliver fault-tolerant universal quantum systems that operate at room temperature and integrate directly with classical HPC and data-center infrastructure.

## Evidence / Context
- Dedalo's key design pillars: photon-loss-tolerant logical qubits, modular photonic hardware, semiconductor-compatible fabrication, and telecom-style interconnects.
- Architecture is explicitly designed for co-location alongside classical supercomputers, not isolated lab environments.
- Photonic approach advantages: room-temperature operation (no dilution refrigerators), compatibility with existing semiconductor fabs, and low-latency fiber interconnects.
- QuiX Quantum previously achieved the first below-threshold error mitigation in photonic quantum computing.
- This is an architecture blueprint / roadmap announcement, not a hardware delivery milestone — commercial deployment timeline not specified.
