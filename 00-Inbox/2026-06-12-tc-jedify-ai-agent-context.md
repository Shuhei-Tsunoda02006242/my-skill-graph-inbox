## Key Claim
Jedify raised a $24M Series A led by Norwest, with Snowflake joining as a strategic investor, to build "context graphs" that connect enterprise data sources so AI agents have the business-specific knowledge — relationships, permissions, terminology, workflows — needed to act reliably.

## Evidence / Context
- Series A led by Norwest, with returning investors S Capital VC and Cerca Partners and new investor Oceans Ventures.
- Snowflake is integrating Jedify's technology with its Cortex AI, Semantic Views, and CoWork products.
- Jedify connects via APIs to databases, data warehouses/lakes, SaaS apps, and BI tools, as well as unstructured sources like reports, documentation, codebases, Slack channels, and meeting recordings.
- Pitch: AI agents need access to relationships, permissions, domain knowledge, workflows, and company-specific terminology to narrow their focus instead of searching across everything.
