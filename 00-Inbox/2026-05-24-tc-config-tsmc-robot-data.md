## Key Claim
Config, a Seoul/San Jose startup building the neutral data layer for robotics foundation models, raised an oversubscribed $27M seed led by Samsung Venture Investment at a >$200M valuation, with every major Korean manufacturer (Samsung, Hyundai, LG, SK) co-investing — a rare unified industrial bet on robot data infrastructure.

## Evidence / Context
- $27M seed round led by Samsung Venture Investment; total raised $35M
- Strategic investors: Hyundai Motor (ZER01NE Ventures), LG Technology Ventures, SKT America — plus angel Pieter Abbeel (Covariant co-founder, UC Berkeley)
- Founded Jan 2025 by CEO Minjoon Seo (ex-Meta researcher, chief scientist at TwelveLabs) and co-founders from Waymo, Google, Naver
- Analogy to TSMC: Config collects and licenses robot training data without competing with robotics manufacturers
- Plans to scale to 1 million hours of collected data (currently operating in Vietnam and Seoul)
- Targeting $10M ARR by end of 2027; launching cloud robot-as-a-service with Config's foundation model
- Current customers: large manufacturers, system integrators, agriculture, defense sectors
- Already generating revenue at seed stage
