## Key Claim
Former White House AI adviser Sriram Krishnan argues the Trump administration will not create an FDA-equivalent regulator for AI and blames the growing public backlash against AI on the industry's own apocalyptic "doomer" messaging.

## Evidence / Context
- Krishnan served as Senior White House Policy Adviser on Artificial Intelligence from January 2025; stepped down effective June 30, 2026
- Tells the Financial Times: "there will not be an FDA for AI" under Trump — framing AI regulation as incompatible with US competitiveness
- Attributes public skepticism toward AI to AI leaders themselves promoting existential-risk narratives
- On July 1 2026, Krishnan appeared on CNBC discussing the Trump administration lifting restrictions on Anthropic model exports — signaling a continued light-touch, pro-deployment stance
- Original reporting: Financial Times, published 2026-07-03 (paywalled)
