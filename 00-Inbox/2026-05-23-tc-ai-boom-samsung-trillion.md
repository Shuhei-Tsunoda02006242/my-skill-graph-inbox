## Key Claim
Samsung reached a $1 trillion market cap as AI demand for High Bandwidth Memory (HBM) chips drove shares up more than 10%, making it only the second Asian company (after TSMC) to cross the threshold.

## Evidence / Context
- Q1 2026 earnings: profits 8× higher year-over-year
- AI data centers require HBM, which Samsung, SK Hynix, and Micron produce — supply is struggling to keep pace with demand
- All three memory giants have redirected investment away from consumer chips toward HBM, which carries substantially higher margins
- Samsung's consumer divisions (phones, TVs) are now paying a steep premium for the same chips fueling Samsung's profits
- Workers threatening an 18-day strike demanding a share of AI-driven profits
- Chip shortage across the semiconductor industry is broadly AI-driven, not a cyclical event
