## Key Claim
Altara raised $7M seed to deploy AI agents that reason across fragmented engineering data — spreadsheets, legacy systems, test logs — to accelerate failure analysis and experimental design in semiconductor, battery, and medical device companies.

## Evidence / Context
- $7M seed round led by Greylock; participation from Neo, BoxGroup, Liquid 2 Ventures, and Jeff Dean
- Founded in 2025 by Eva Tuecke (particle physics research at Fermilab, SpaceX) and Catherine Yeo (former AI engineer at Warp)
- Target industries: semiconductors, batteries, advanced materials, medical devices
- Approach: plug-in intelligence layer on top of existing data systems, not a rip-and-replace; AI agents handle experimental design and failure analysis workflows
- Published May 5, 2026
