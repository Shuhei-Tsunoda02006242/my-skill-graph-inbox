## Key Claim
Nvidia is pitching its new Arm-based Vera CPU to Chinese cloud and data-center customers, with orders open now and shipments targeted as early as August, as a way to regain ground in China after its advanced GPUs were effectively shut out by export controls.

## Evidence / Context
- The 88-core Vera CPU, unveiled in March 2026, is part of Nvidia's Vera Rubin platform (paired with Rubin GPUs) and is now in full production.
- The China pitch isolates the CPU from the GPU specifically because the GPU component would likely draw regulatory scrutiny.
- One major Chinese cloud provider is reportedly considering an order of 300+ servers, each with two Vera processors.
- CEO Jensen Huang has said Nvidia's GPU market share in China "has effectively fallen to zero" amid stalled H200 shipments.
