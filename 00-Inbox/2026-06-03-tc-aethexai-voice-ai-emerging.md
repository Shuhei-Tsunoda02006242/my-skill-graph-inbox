## Key Claim
AethexAI built its own small language model and orchestration layer from scratch to deliver low-latency voice AI that handles local dialects of English, French, and Arabic — solving a problem that existing off-the-shelf solutions failed to crack for African and Middle Eastern call centers.

## Evidence / Context
- Founders: CEO Mariama Diallo (ex-Goldman Sachs, ModelML) and CTO Ayooluwa Odemuyiwa (Caltech, ex-Meta, Stanford MBA)
- Raised $3M pre-seed led by 4DX Ventures (specialist Africa/emerging-market fund)
- Core technical moat: custom small model + orchestration layer tuned for local dialect processing and low latency
- Identified opportunity after Egyptian call center rolled back an automated system due to poor results; African support centers cited cost and difficulty finding engineers
- Founded 2025; total funding $3M
