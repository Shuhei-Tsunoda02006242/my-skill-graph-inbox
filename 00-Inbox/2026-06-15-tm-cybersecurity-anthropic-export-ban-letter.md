## Key Claim
Over 40 cybersecurity leaders, organized by former Facebook CSO Alex Stamos, sent a letter to Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick and National Cyber Director Sean Cairncross urging the Trump administration to reverse its export-control ban on foreign access to Anthropic's Mythos 5 and Fable 5 models, arguing it weakens defenders without reducing real risk.

## Evidence / Context
- The ban was announced via a letter Lutnick sent to Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei on Friday, blocking Mythos 5/Fable 5 access outside the US and to all foreign persons within the US
- Signatories include Katie Moussouris (Luta Security), Rachel Tobac (SocialProof Security), Chris Wysopal (Veracode), Paul Vixie, Joe Levy (Sophos CEO), and Aaron Grattafiori (Nvidia security researcher)
- Letter states the action "has taken the best models away from defenders, created market uncertainty, and risked America's AI leadership without any real risk to justify it"
- Anthropic suggests the ban may stem from a report describing a jailbreak that lets Fable unlock Mythos-level capabilities; signatories argue the underlying issue exists across other leading AI models too
