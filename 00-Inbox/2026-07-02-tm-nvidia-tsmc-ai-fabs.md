## Key Claim
TSMC is deploying NVIDIA's AI and accelerated computing stack across the full semiconductor manufacturing lifecycle—from computational lithography and transistor simulation to defect inspection and factory scheduling—marking the deepest known integration of AI into leading-edge fab operations.

## Evidence / Context
- CUDA-X libraries (cuLitho, cuEST) deliver **20–50% lithography simulation gains** and **50x faster transistor simulations** for TSMC workflows.
- TSMC is using NVIDIA Metropolis and TAO Toolkit for vision-AI-based defect inspection at nanometer scale, reducing the need for repeated labeling and retraining.
- TSMC is exploring NVIDIA Omniverse to build **FabTwin**, a virtual fab environment for testing process tool layouts and simulation workflows before physical implementation.
- Advanced process control and fab operations optimization are also covered, targeting improvements in turnaround time, energy efficiency, yield, and productivity.
- Announced June 1, 2026.
