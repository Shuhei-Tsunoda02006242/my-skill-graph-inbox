## Key Claim
AI inference startup Baseten is close to finalizing a $1.5B round at a $13B valuation — just five months after its $300M Series E at a $5B valuation, a 160% jump — signaling that inference infrastructure remains one of the highest-conviction bets in venture.

## Evidence / Context
- Round co-led by Spark Capital, Sands Capital, Altimeter Capital, and Wellington Management
- Deal uses a split-priced structure: some investors at $13B valuation, others at $11B
- Previous $300M Series E closed approximately January 2026 at $5B
- Baseten serves AI inference for enterprise customers including OpenAI and AWS
- Context: AI inference market exploding as model serving becomes the compute bottleneck post-training; competitors include Modal Labs (reportedly at $2.5B valuation) and RadixArk (SGLang spin-out at $400M)
