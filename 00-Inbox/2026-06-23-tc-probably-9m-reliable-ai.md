## Key Claim
Probably raised a $9M seed co-led by Andreessen Horowitz (a16z) and Accel, betting that the fix for AI unreliability is NOT smarter/bigger models but weaker, more constrained ones that can achieve 99.99%-level accuracy.

## Evidence / Context
- $9M seed co-led by a16z and Accel
- Core thesis: while the industry scales models up to reduce hallucinations, Probably argues simpler deterministic-style constraints on weaker models can eliminate errors at the output layer
- Problem framing: RAG systems hallucinate, agents break in subtle and expensive ways, and critical workflows still rely heavily on human QA
- Root cause identified: "data entropy" — unstructured, messy inputs causing downstream AI failures
- Targets enterprise workflows where AI errors are commercially unacceptable
- Aligns with broader 2026 industry shift from scaling laws toward reliability engineering
