## Key Claim
Baidu's AI chip unit Kunlunxin is targeting a $50 billion Hong Kong IPO — a valuation that eclipses parent Baidu's own ~$38B market cap and has grown 16x from $3B in July 2025. Prospective investors are reportedly required to purchase Kunlunxin semiconductors worth 3–7x the value of their intended equity stake.

## Evidence / Context
- Valuation trajectory: ~$3B (Jul 2025) → ~$12.8B (May 2026, TrendForce) → ~$14.7B (Jun 2026, SCMP) → $50B target (Jun 29 report from The Information)
- Baidu HK shares jumped +7% on the day of the report
- Filed confidentially for HK listing in January 2026; pursuing dual listing on Shanghai's STAR Market
- Unusual "chip bundle" condition: investors asked to commit to buying Kunlunxin semiconductors at 3–7x their planned equity investment
- Kunlunxin founded 2010 as Baidu's internal AI chip project; develops Kunlun series of AI inference chips
- Context: China's domestic AI chip sector under US export restrictions on Nvidia H100/H200/A100; Kunlunxin fills substitution demand
