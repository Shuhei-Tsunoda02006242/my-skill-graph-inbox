## Key Claim
Bret Taylor's Sierra raised a $950M Series E at a $15.8B valuation, reaching $150M ARR in just 8 quarters and claiming over 40% of the Fortune 50 as customers — an unprecedented growth trajectory for enterprise software.

## Evidence / Context
- Round led by Tiger Global and Google Ventures (GV), with Benchmark, Sequoia, Greenoaks participating
- Co-founded by Bret Taylor (OpenAI chairman, former Salesforce co-CEO) and Clay Bavor (former Google executive)
- $150M ARR achieved in 8 quarters — described as unprecedented in traditional software
- 40%+ of the Fortune 50 are customers (started with just 4 design partners)
- Positioning: "global standard" for AI-powered customer experiences, built atop foundational models from OpenAI and Anthropic
