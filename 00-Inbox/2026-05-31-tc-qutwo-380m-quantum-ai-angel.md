## Key Claim
Finnish AI lab QuTwo, founded by former AMD Silo AI CEO Peter Sarlin, raised €25M ($29M) at a €325M ($380M) valuation in an angel round, positioning its QuTwo OS orchestration layer as the enterprise on-ramp to quantum computing before fault-tolerant hardware exists.

## Evidence / Context
- Raised €25M (~$29M) at €325M (~$380M) valuation in an angel round deliberately avoiding VC and strategic investors
- QuTwo OS routes enterprise workloads across classical, quantum-inspired, and hybrid architectures
- Already secured ~$23M in committed revenue via design partnerships, including Zalando AI assistants
- Angel backers include Yuri Milner, Xavier Niel, Nico Rosberg, Dieter Schwarz, Niklas Zennström, and founders from Hugging Face, Skype, Supercell, Wolt, Miro, Legora
- Sarlin chose angel round partly due to geopolitical concerns about European tech sovereignty
