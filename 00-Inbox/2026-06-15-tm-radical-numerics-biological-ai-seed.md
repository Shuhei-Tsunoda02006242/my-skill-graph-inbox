## Key Claim
Radical Numerics emerged from stealth with a $50M seed round led by Emergence Capital to build "general biological intelligence" — AI models that learn directly and jointly across DNA, RNA, proteins, and other biological data types, rather than analyzing each in isolation.

## Evidence / Context
- Founded by Eric Nguyen (CEO, Stanford Bioengineering/AI PhD), Michael Poli (Chief AI Scientist, Liquid AI founding team), Stefano Massaroli (President, postdoc with Yoshua Bengio, Liquid AI founding team), and Armin Thomas (CTO, Stanford postdoc with Chris Ré)
- Founding team previously built Evo, a generative genomics model capable of reading and generating DNA sequences at scale
- Round included Obvious Ventures, Triatomic Capital, Factory, and First Spark Ventures, with pre-seed backing from Patrick Collison
- Stated goal is to unify biological data that has traditionally been analyzed separately, opening new approaches to cancer diagnostics, drug target identification, disease detection, and biosecurity
