## Key Claim
Brett Adcock's Hark raised $700M at a $6B post-money valuation to build a fully vertically integrated AI personal assistant — its own foundation models, software stack, and custom hardware — positioning itself as a platform rather than a single-layer AI product.

## Evidence / Context
- $700M Series A led by Parkway Venture Capital; $6B post-money valuation
- Strategic chip-industry backers: Nvidia, AMD Ventures, Intel Capital, Qualcomm Ventures
- Adcock seeded the company with $100M of his own capital in late 2025
- 70 employees; operates an in-house data center running Nvidia B200 GPUs
- First multimodal models expected to ship summer 2026; will power a personal AI platform interoperating with existing apps and services
- Adcock previously founded robotics company Figure.AI and electric aircraft startup Archer
