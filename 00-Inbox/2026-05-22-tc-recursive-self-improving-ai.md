## Key Claim
Recursive Superintelligence emerged from stealth with $650M at a $4.65B valuation to build an AI that can autonomously identify its own weaknesses and redesign itself without human involvement, aiming to automate the full loop of ideation, implementation, and validation of AI research.

## Evidence / Context
- $650M raised; $4.65B valuation; backed by Nvidia, GV (Google Ventures), Greycroft
- Founded by Richard Socher (You.com, Salesforce AI) alongside Peter Norvig (former Google Research director)
- Team drawn from Meta AI (FAIR), Google DeepMind, OpenAI, Salesforce AI
- Key researchers: Yuandong Tian (Meta FAIR director), Tim Rocktäschel (UCL/DeepMind), Alexey Dosovitskiy (Vision Transformer co-author)
- Technical approach: open-endedness as a mechanism for recursive self-improvement — AI autonomously cycles through ideation → implementation → validation without human oversight
- Company says first product ships "within quarters, not years"
