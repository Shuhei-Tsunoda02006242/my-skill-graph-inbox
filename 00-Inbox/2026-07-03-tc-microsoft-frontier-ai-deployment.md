## Key Claim
Microsoft launched Microsoft Frontier Company, a new $2.5 billion operating unit that embeds 6,000 engineers and industry experts inside enterprise customers to co-design, deploy, and continuously improve AI systems — a model called forward-deployed engineering (FDE).

## Evidence / Context
- Announced July 2, 2026 by Commercial Business CEO Judson Althoff; led by Rodrigo Kede Lima (formerly president of Microsoft Asia)
- Unit consolidates existing Microsoft FDEs, technical consultants, support staff, and salespeople with deep industry domain knowledge
- Named anchor customers: London Stock Exchange Group, Unilever, Land O'Lakes, Accenture
- Amazon committed $1 billion to a similar FDE initiative two days earlier; OpenAI and Anthropic launched comparable programs in May 2026
- Competitive pressure point: the race is no longer just about who has the best model, but who can demonstrate measurable ROI from AI deployment at enterprise scale
- Microsoft already has FDE relationships with Accenture, Capgemini, EY, KPMG, and PwC — the new unit formalizes and scales that approach with direct Microsoft headcount
