## Key Claim
Sarvam AI, an Indian foundation-model startup focused on LLMs and speech technology for Indian languages, raised $234M in the first close of its Series B at a $1.5B post-money valuation, becoming India's newest AI unicorn.

## Evidence / Context
- Round led by HCLTech with a $150M investment, alongside Bessemer Venture Partners and existing backers Khosla Ventures and Peak XV Partners.
- Founded in 2023 by Vivek Raghavan and Pratyush Kumar; products are deployed across banking, insurance, government services, and defense in India.
- Fresh capital will fund training of Sarvam's next frontier model, with focus on agentic, coding, and cybersecurity use cases, and to expand compute access.
- Sarvam is targeting a total raise of $300M for this Series B round (this is the first close).
