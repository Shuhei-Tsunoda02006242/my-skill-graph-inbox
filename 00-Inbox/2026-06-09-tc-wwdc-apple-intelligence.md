## Key Claim
Apple's WWDC 2026 overhauled Siri with Google Gemini under the hood and expanded Apple Intelligence with cross-app context awareness across Messages, Mail, Phone, and Safari — representing Apple's deepest AI integration yet and CEO Tim Cook's final WWDC before handing the company to John Ternus on September 1.

## Evidence / Context
- New Siri powered by Google Gemini: more conversational, visual intelligence support, available as a standalone app
- Apple Intelligence new features: Safari tab management, one-tap password updates, cross-app context (e.g., Phone app reads context from Mail/Messages mid-call), AI reply suggestions in Messages
- iOS 27 performance: photos load 70% faster, AirDrop 80% faster, improved CPU scheduler for multitasking
- iOS 27 supports all devices from iPhone 11 onward
- Tim Cook's last WWDC; John Ternus (SVP Hardware Engineering) takes over as CEO September 1
