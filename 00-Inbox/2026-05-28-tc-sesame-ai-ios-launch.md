## Key Claim
Sesame — founded by Oculus co-founders — launched a public iOS app in 39 countries featuring four distinct AI agents with unique voices, personalities, and memory, marking the first consumer milestone on its path to AI smartglasses in 2027.

## Evidence / Context
- Founded by Oculus co-founders (VR company sold to Meta)
- Previously raised $250M (October 2025), backed by Sequoia Capital
- iOS app available in 39 countries, free at launch
- Four agents: Maya, Miles, Simone, Charlie — each with distinct voice, personality, and persistent memory
- Features: search cards with image results, notes capture, texting mode, deep-dive results
- Full app launch follows a beta period in which user feedback shaped feature development
- Smartglasses product planned for 2027; iOS app is explicitly the first step
