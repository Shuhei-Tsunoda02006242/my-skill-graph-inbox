## Key Claim
Snowflake signed a new $6 billion five-year deal with AWS focused on AI infrastructure, including Amazon's Graviton custom chips and cloud-based GPUs — its AWS commitment has grown 5x since its 2020 IPO.

## Evidence / Context
- $6B over 5 years, up from $2.5B in 2023 and $1.2B at 2020 IPO
- Snowflake customer AWS spending doubled in 2025 alone to $2B for the year
- Deal centers on Amazon Graviton general-purpose chips and cloud GPUs for AI workloads
- Prior month: AWS supplied Graviton chips to Meta after Meta's $10B Google Cloud deal
- Snowflake shares rose ~35% on the same earnings beat that accompanied this announcement
