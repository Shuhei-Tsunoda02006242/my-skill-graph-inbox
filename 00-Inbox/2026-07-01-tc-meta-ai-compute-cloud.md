## Key Claim
Meta is launching "Meta Compute," a cloud infrastructure business to sell surplus AI compute and models to outside companies, competing directly with AWS, Google Cloud, Azure, CoreWeave, and Nebius.

## Evidence / Context
- Meta guided $115B–$135B capex for 2026; selling idle capacity is a primary ROI lever
- Announcement drove Meta shares up ~8%; CoreWeave and Nebius stocks cratered immediately after
- Both CoreWeave and Nebius had signed multi-billion-dollar contracts to supply Meta with compute it now plans to sell back
- Mirrors SpaceX's Colossus 1 compute monetization via Anthropic, Google, and Reflection AI deals ($150M/month)
- Zuckerberg had flagged this possibility in May; confirmation came July 1, 2026
- Meta plans to sell both raw compute access and access to its AI models
