## Key Claim
Anthropic released Claude Fable 5, the first publicly available Mythos-class model, days after warning that AI systems may soon achieve recursive self-improvement; the model ships with hard domain blocks on cybersecurity, biology, chemistry, and distillation — falling back to Opus 4.8 in those areas.

## Evidence / Context
- Priced at $10/M input tokens and $50/M output tokens — double Opus 4.8 pricing
- External bug bounty ran 1,000+ hours with zero universal jailbreaks found before launch
- Free on Pro/Max/Team/Enterprise plans until June 22; moves to usage-credits on June 23
- Available on Amazon Bedrock at launch
- High-risk domain blocks (cybersecurity, biology, chemistry, distillation) are hard classifier gates, not soft guidelines
