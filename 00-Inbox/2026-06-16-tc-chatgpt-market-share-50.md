## Key Claim
For the first time since launch, ChatGPT's global market share fell below 50%, landing at 46.4% as of end-May 2026, according to Sensor Tower's 2026 AI Status Report — signaling a genuine shift from a one-player AI assistant market to a multi-player race.

## Evidence / Context
- ChatGPT: 46.4% share (down from >50% through January 2026); still 1.1B monthly users
- Gemini: 27.7% (Google's distribution flywheel at work)
- Claude: 10.3% (Anthropic climbing ahead of its IPO)
- Grok, Perplexity, DeepSeek, Meta AI: each <5% individually
- Data source: Sensor Tower "2026 AI Status Report"
