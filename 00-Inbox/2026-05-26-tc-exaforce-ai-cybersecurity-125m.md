## Key Claim
Exaforce's AI-native security platform maps every event, identity, config, and cloud activity into a real-time knowledge graph, with AI agents (Exabots) completing attack investigation in under a minute — compressing work that previously took human analysts hours.

## Evidence / Context
- $125M Series B (total raised: $200M), valued at $725M; round led by HarbourVest, Peak XV, Mayfield, Khosla Ventures, Seligman Ventures
- Founded 3 years ago; raised $75M Series A just one year prior
- ~20 enterprise customers including Replit and Guardant Health; targeting 40–50 by year-end
- Introduced "vibe hunting": natural language queries let security teams investigate threats based on hunches
- Competes with Palo Alto Networks, CrowdStrike (incumbents) and Dropzone AI, 7AI, Prophet Security (startups)
