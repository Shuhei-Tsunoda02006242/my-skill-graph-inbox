## Key Claim
Coralogix raised a $200M Series F at a $1.6B valuation to build the monitoring/observability layer for AI agents, betting that the explosive growth of autonomous software systems creates an entirely new category of operational visibility needs.

## Evidence / Context
- Series F led by Advent and Canada Pension Plan Investment Board (CPPIB); also Greenfield Partners and Brighton Park Capital
- Total raised to date: $550M; previous round was $115M Series E just 11 months prior
- 5,000+ enterprise customers including IBM, Tradeweb, JFrog
- >60% revenue growth year-over-year; ~30 customers spending >$1M annually
- >50% of enterprise customers now use Coralogix's AI agent "Olly" or their own AI via CLI/agentic interfaces
- Platform collects logs, metrics, and traces; agents use it to investigate incidents autonomously
- Funding earmarked for AI-focused products, security offerings, and global expansion
