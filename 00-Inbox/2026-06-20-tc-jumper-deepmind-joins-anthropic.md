## Key Claim
John Jumper, Nobel laureate in Chemistry (co-winner 2024 for AlphaFold) and nearly 9-year veteran of Google DeepMind, is joining Anthropic — part of a wave of top AI scientists defecting between frontier labs the same week.

## Evidence / Context
- Jumper led the AlphaFold team at DeepMind and co-won the 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry with Demis Hassabis for protein structure prediction
- Announced departure after "nearly 9 years" via a post on X, crediting Hassabis for taking a chance on him "just six months after finishing my PhD"
- Move mirrors Noam Shazeer's departure from Google to OpenAI the same week — a broader talent exodus from Google across both Anthropic and OpenAI simultaneously
- Anthropic reported it expected its first profitable quarter as of May 2026
