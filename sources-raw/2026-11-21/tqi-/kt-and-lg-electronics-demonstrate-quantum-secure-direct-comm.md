Insider Brief
- KT and LG Electronics have demonstrated a quantum secure direct communication (QSDC) system that successfully transmitted and reconstructed image and text data.
- KT developed the transmission and receiving equipment and communication procedures, while LG Electronics assessed the system’s security and researched ways to reduce component size and simplify its design.
- The companies filed 13 patent applications during development and plan further work on transmission speed, system stability and verification in fibre-optic networks.
- Photo Credits: KT
Image and text data were sent and then recovered at the receiving end using a quantum secure direct communication (QSDC) system that KT developed jointly with LG Electronics, KT said on Thursday, according to Digital Today.
Digital Today described QSDC as a next-generation quantum communications technology that carries the information itself as a quantum state. Quantum key distribution (QKD), by contrast, shares the keys used to encrypt information. The outlet reported that KT widened the scope of its quantum communications technology by securing QSDC after its existing QKD capabilities.
In the demonstration, KT converted 1,024-bit image data into quantum signals, sent them, and reconstructed the original data at the receiving end. KT also sent and restored text data with the same system.
KT and LG Electronics signed a memorandum of understanding (MoU) last year to develop next-generation quantum internet technology, and related research has continued since then, according to Digital Today. KT built the QSDC transmitting and receiving equipment and the communications procedures. It also implemented technology that turns data into a quantum state, transmits it and restores the original information. LG Electronics analysed and verified the system’s security and carried out research on making related components smaller and the system structure simpler.
The companies filed 13 patent applications during development, 7 from KT and 6 from LG Electronics. KT plans to raise QSDC transmission speed and system stability and to develop the technology so it can be used in actual communications networks. It will also pursue verification in fibre-optic-based networks. LG Electronics will keep working with KT on the quantum internet and expand research into distributed quantum computing that uses quantum processing units (QPUs), as reported by Digital Today.
Hyo-jun Lim (임효준), head of the Next-Generation Computing Research Center at LG Electronics, said, “QSDC is one of the important technologies for advancing into quantum networks.” He added, “We will continue cooperation with KT on next-generation quantum internet technology and pursue research not only on advancing the technology but also on areas such as QPU distributed quantum computing.”
Jong-sik Lee (이종식), head of the Future Network Lab in KT’s network division, said, “This technology development is meaningful in that we secured end-to-end quantum communications technology that directly encodes actual information into a quantum state, transmits it and restores it.” He added, “We will expand technology verification to real network environments and continue technology cooperation in the next-generation quantum internet field.”
