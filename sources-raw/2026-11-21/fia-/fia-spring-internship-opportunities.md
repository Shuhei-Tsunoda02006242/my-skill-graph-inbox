The Fusion Industry Association (FIA) is hiring two paid, part-time interns this spring with in-person work in Washington, D.C.
The FIA is the unified voice of the fusion industry, working to transform the energy system with commercially viable fusion power. It represents the interests of its members: the private companies that are commercializing clean, safe, available fusion energy.
We are seeking two motivated individuals to intern part-time with us: a Public Policy Intern reporting directly to the Membership Manager and an Events and Membership Intern reporting directly to the Events Coordinator. Applicants are expected to be located in Washington, DC, and actively pursuing a degree. Qualifications, responsibilities, and employment dates differ between the positions; please see below for more information.
Public Policy Intern
Key Responsibilities:
- Writing blog posts based on FIA priorities and developing fusion news and policy activity.
- Assisting in social media outreach (X, LinkedIn, Bluesky, YouTube, Instagram) and developing assets.
- Opportunity to develop a self-led project, based on personal and professional interests, related to FIA priorities in government partnerships, regulations, and growing the fusion energy economy.
- Supporting Capitol Hill advocacy and outreach efforts.
- Contributing to the overall success of the FIA and supporting staff operations across various initiatives.
Requirements:
- Strong writing and communications skills
- Ability to manage multiple projects and work independently
- Must be located in DC
- Must be pursuing a degree (bachelor’s, master’s, etc.)
Dates: We are looking for someone to start in January and work through May.
If you are passionate about fusion energy and are interested in learning more about fusion policy, regulatory activity, and industry developments, we encourage you to apply for this opportunity. (Physics and scientific background is not required.)
TO APPLY: Applicants for the role should send a 1.) cover letter, 2.) writing sample, and 3.) resume to Sara Eaves, Membership Manager, at Sara@FusionIndustryAssociation.org. Please be prepared to supply references on request.
Events and Membership Intern
Key Responsibilities:
- Assisting with event logistics and coordination, including attendee management, online logistics (registration page, website, and event app set up) and venue set-up
- Serving as a secondary point of contact for event-related questions
- Assisting with membership material development and distribution
- Contributing to FIA membership database organization
- Opportunity to develop a self-led project, based on personal and professional interests, related to FIA priorities.
- Contributing to the overall success of the FIA and supporting staff operations across various initiatives.
Requirements:
- Experience in event coordination, paid or volunteer
- Strong writing and communication skills, especially for member or attendee-facing interactions
- Strong attention to detail
- Ability to manage multiple projects and work independently
- Experience with events or project management software preferred but not required (Cvent, AirTable, Whova, EventsAir, etc)
- Must be located in DC
- Must be pursuing a degree (bachelor’s, master’s, etc.)
Dates: We are looking for someone to start in January and work through July 31.
If you are passionate about event logistics and coordination and are interested in learning more about fusion policy, regulatory activity, and industry developments, we encourage you to apply for this opportunity. (Physics and scientific background is not required.)
TO APPLY: Applicants for the role should send a cover letter and resume to Raquel Mac Donald, Events Coordinator, rmacdonald@FusionIndustryAssociation.org. Please include in your cover letter a description of an event you coordinated or assisted with. Please be prepared to supply references on request.
