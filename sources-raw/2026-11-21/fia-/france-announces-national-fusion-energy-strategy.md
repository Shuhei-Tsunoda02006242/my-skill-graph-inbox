On November 16, Philippe Baptiste, France’s Minister of Higher Education, Research and Innovation, presented the country’s National Fusion Energy Strategy for 2026 to 2035, marking an important step in recognizing and prioritizing fusion as an industry.
Coming ahead of the anticipated release of an EU fusion strategy, France’s strategy outlines the country’s priorities for commercial fusion. It calls for strengthening the domestic fusion industry; advancing the supply chain, critical technologies, and workforce; and supporting the country’s role on fusion in the broader European Union.
Building on existing fusion research infrastructure, including ITER and WEST, the strategy supports preparations for pilot plant deployment and greater coordination between public research institutions and industry. However, it does not introduce new funding commitments. Industry stakeholders have emphasized the need to translate these priorities into concrete investments and closer public-private collaboration.
FIA welcomes the French government’s prioritization and recognition of fusion as an industry, and looks forward to supporting industry consultations and stronger partnerships between sectors as fusion moves towards deployment.
