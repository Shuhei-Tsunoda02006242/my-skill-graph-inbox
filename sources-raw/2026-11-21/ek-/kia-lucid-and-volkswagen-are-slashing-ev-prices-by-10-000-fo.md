Forget waiting in line for a discounted TV or other gadget you probably don’t need. If you’ve been looking to go electric, Black Friday may be the time to do it. Several brands, including Kia, Lucid, and Volkswagen, are offering $10,000 or more off select EVs right now.
Which EVs are on sale for Black Friday this year?
While electric vehicle sales have slowed in the US after last year’s rush of buyers trying to claim the $7,500 tax credit before it expired, the market is bouncing back.
Nearly 263,000 EVs were sold in the US in the third quarter, according to Kelley Blue Book, marking the most since the record-setting third quarter of 2025.
With new models hitting the market, prices have also come down over the past year. The average transaction price (ATP) for a new EV in October was $56,221, down 2.9% from a year ago.
For Black Friday, several automakers are sweetening the deal this year, offering a generous $10,000 or more off select EVs.
Lucid
Lucid launched its Black Friday offers this week. Through November 30, you can score a $15,000 Black Friday Credit on a new 2027 Lucid Gravity Touring.
Prefer a sedan? Lucid is also offering a $10,000 Black Friday Credit on a new 2027 Lucid Air Touring. To take advantage of the offer, you must pay cash or use third-party financing.
The 2027 Lucid Gravity Touring starts at $79,900 and offers up to 337 miles of EPA-estimated range. It’s available to lease for as low as $749 a month. The offer is for a 36-month lease with $6,249 due at signing.
Lucid’s electric sedan, the 2027 Air Touring, is priced from $79,900 and offers up to 431 miles of range.
Both the Gravity and Air can add up to 200 miles of range in under 12 minutes with 400 kW DC fast charging.
Lucid’s Black Friday offers end on November 30, 2026.
Kia
Kia is offering $10,000 off several electric vehicles for Black Friday, including the three-row EV9 and the Niro EV.
The 2026 Kia EV9 is available with a $10,000 Customer Cash discount that can be applied toward the purchase price. With a $10,000 discount, prices for the base EV9 Light RWD trim can fall to as low as $44,900 ($54,900 starting MSRP).
Kia is also promoting a lease deal on the 2026 EV9 Light Long Range RWD for $339/month for 36 months, with $4,999 due at signing.
If you opt to finance, you can get 0% APR for 60 months, plus $5,000 in APR Bonus Cash across the entire 2026 EV9 lineup.
The 2026 Kia Niro EV is also available with a $10,000 Customer Cash discount, knocking the price down to as low as $29,700 ($39,700 MSRP).
With leases starting at just $289/month for 36 months (with $3,999 due at signing), the 2026 Niro EV Wind FWD is about as cheap a lease as you can get nowadays, gas or electric.
The Niro EV is also available with 0% APR for up to 72 months, including a $3,500 APR Bonus Cash discount.
While it isn’t $10,000 off, it is worth mentioning that Kia now sells the smaller EV3 electric SUV. The 2027 Kia EV3 (see our review) starts at just $28,890, making it one of the most affordable EVs you can get in the US, alongside the new Chevy Bolt and Nissan LEAF.
Kia is offering a $1,500 Customer Cash discount on the 2027 EV3, which still makes it cheaper to buy outright than the Niro EV, even with incentives.
The 2027 Kia EV3 Wind FWD is available for lease starting at $349/month for 36 months, with $3,999 due at signing. Kia is offering 0.90% APR for 48 months on all 2027 EV3 trims.
Kia’s current deals end on January 04, 2027.
Volkswagen ID.4
As it prepares to introduce its replacement in 2027, Volkswagen introduced a $10,000 Customer Bonus for the 2026 ID.4 electric SUV.
Since the ID.4 is discontinued after the 2026 model year, it might not be so easy to find one. Volkswagen said the remaining inventory is very limited.
If you can find one, Volkswagen will apply the $10,000 discount when you purchase or lease a new 2026 ID.4 through a participating dealer.
Volkswagen’s current ID.4 offer runs until November 30, 2026. You can read more about its replacement, the ID. Tiguan here.
