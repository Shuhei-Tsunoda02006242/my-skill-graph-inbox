The new Renault Twingo E-Tech is among the most affordable vehicles, electric or gas-powered, starting at just £14,745 in the UK, or under $20,000.
Renault Twingo E-Tech prices and specs
Another affordable, practical EV the US will never see. Renault’s new Twingo E-Tech is an electric remake of its iconic 1990s city car.
The retro-looking EV went on sale in Europe earlier this year, starting at under €20,000 ($22,000), before government incentives. In places like Germany, it starts at about €13,990 ($15,500), including the €6,000 ($6,600) federal EV grant.
In the UK, Renault announced that prices for the Twingo E-Tech will start at just £14,745, or about $19,500. That includes the government’s Electric Clean Car Grant (ECG) of £3,750 ($4,900).
At that price, Renault’s new electric car is among the most affordable vehicles available, including gas-powered cars.
It’s cheaper than the gas-powered Dacia Sandero (£14,765) and BYD’s cheapest EV, the Dolphin Surf (£18,675). Only a few cars are lower-priced, including the Dacia Spring (£11,990) and Leapmotor T03 (£12,995).
What do you get for the price? At just 3,790 mm (149.2″) long, the electric city car is a bit smaller than the Hyundai Inster and the BYD Dolphin Surf.
For those in the US, that’s even smaller than the first-generation Chevy Bolt EV, which measured 4,145 mm (163.2″) in length.
Despite its compact size, the “cheeky, practical” 5-door city car, as Renault calls it, offers up to 360 liters of boot capacity. It also features independent, sliding 50/50 folding rear seats to haul bigger items like a surfboard.
With up to 50 kW DC fast charging, Renault said the Twingo E-Tech can charge from 15% to 80% in 28 minutes.
The base Renault Twingo E-Tech Evolution, priced from £14,745 ($19,500), uses a 27.5 kWh LFP battery and an 80 hp (60 kW) motor, delivering up to 163 miles of WLTP range.
The entry-level version features 16″ steel wheels, a 7″ driver display, 10″ navigation screen, and ADAS features, including cruise control and lane-keep assist.
Renault will offer three Twingo E-Tech trims in total. The mid-range Techno trim gains 18″ wheels, Google built-in with Renault’s Reno avatar, a reversing camera, automatic climate control, a hands-free key card, One Pedal driving, and heated front seats. It starts at £16,245 ($21,500), including the £3,750 ($4,900) ECG.
With Google built in, drivers can easily find and navigate to charging stations via Google Maps, use Google Gemini AI virtual assistance, or access over 100 apps, including Waze and Amazon Music.

The top-spec Twingo E-Tech Launch Edition, priced from £17,645 ($23,200), including the £3,750 ($4,900) ECG, adds a suite of ADAS features, including blind-spot warning, rear cross-traffic alert and hands-free parking.
Inside, the Launch Edition features a special mottled textile upholstery with red stitching and premium floor mats.
Renault is offering a special promo for those who buy a new Twingo E-Tech through Mobilize Financial Services on a PCP or HP agreement by June 30, 2027, which includes two years’ free servicing.
Interested buyers can purchase the Twingo R Pass for £100, giving them priority access to ordering, a mini Twingo E-Tech model, and a green Renault key cover.
