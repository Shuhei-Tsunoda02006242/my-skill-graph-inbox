Oura deal may complicate Lilly’s GLP-1 telehealth ties
Eli Lilly already sends patients looking for a GLP-1 prescription to Counsel Health through its pharmacy platform LillyDirect. Now, smart ring maker Oura is buying Counsel, which could give Lilly a financial interest in a telehealth company it promotes. Lilly has agreed to buy $50 million in Oura stock if certain conditions are met, though the drugmaker says those conditions haven’t been met yet, STAT’s Elaine Chen writes.
