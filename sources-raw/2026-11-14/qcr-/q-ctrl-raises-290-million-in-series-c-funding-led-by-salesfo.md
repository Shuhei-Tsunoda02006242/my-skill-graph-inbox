Quantum infrastructure software provider Q-CTRL has secured US$290 million (A$415 million) in an oversubscribed Series C funding round led by Salesforce Ventures. Expanded twice to accommodate institutional demand, the round values the Sydney- and U.S.-based company above $1 billion and represents the largest private capital raise recorded for a software-focused quantum technology firm.

Q-CTRL plans to allocate the new capital toward expanding its AI-driven control software stack and scaling global commercial deployments across finance, energy, defense, and aerospace. Its infrastructure software operates across two primary product lines: Fire Opal and Boulder Opal for suppressing noise and automating calibration in gate-model quantum computers, and Ironstone Opal for GPS-denied quantum magnetic navigation.
The financing follows a series of technical and regulatory milestones for the company, including RTCA DO-160 airworthiness qualification for Ironstone Opal across commercial aviation and unmanned platforms, as well as the deployment of its Fire Opal Dynamics Simulator inside the IBM Quantum Functions Catalog. Backed by new regional institutional partners, Q-CTRL also plans to accelerate its commercial expansion across Japan’s industrial and high-performance computing sectors.
