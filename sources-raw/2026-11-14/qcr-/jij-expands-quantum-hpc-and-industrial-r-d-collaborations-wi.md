Quantum and mathematical optimization software provider JIJ Inc. has announced three research and industrial collaborations spanning hybrid high-performance computing, power-grid optimization, and computational biology. Highlighted by the selection of a joint proposal with Mitsubishi Electric Corporation for Japan’s JHPC-quantum Test User Program, the initiatives pair JIJ’s hybrid algorithmic stack with domain-specific engineering and life-science workflows.
| JIJ Quantum-HPC & Enterprise Research Initiatives |  |  | 
|---|---|---|
| Partner / Program | Application Domain | Technical Scope & Milestone | 
| Mitsubishi Electric (JHPC-quantum) | Thermal-fluid engineering simulation | Evaluates quantum-classical hybrid linear solvers on RIKEN and SoftBank’s integrated QC-HPC platform. | 
| Q-CTRL (NEDO Challenge Season 1) | Electrical power-grid optimization | Received Outstanding Research Award (Society 5.0) for hybrid distribution network reconfiguration on QPUs. | 
| Tinuviel Therapeutics (Wellcome Leap FORM) | Predictive gut microbiome modeling | Develops constrained dynamical optimization models to assess early-life microbiome resilience and interventions. | 
Under the JHPC-quantum project, jointly developed by RIKEN and SoftBank Corp. as part of Japan’s NEDO Post-5G infrastructure initiative, Mitsubishi Electric serves as lead proposer alongside JIJ to evaluate hybrid linear-system solvers for large-scale thermal-fluid simulations used in transportation, electronics, and climate-control engineering.
In parallel, JIJ and quantum infrastructure software developer Q-CTRL were recognized with an Outstanding Research Award in the Society 5.0 category at NEDO Challenge Season 1 for testing hybrid distribution network reconfiguration algorithms on physical quantum processors. JIJ also formalized a collaboration initiated in April 2026 with precision health firm Tinuviel Therapeutics under Wellcome Leap’s Foundations of a Resilient Microbiome (FORM) program to model microbial community dynamics and recovery under external stressors.
