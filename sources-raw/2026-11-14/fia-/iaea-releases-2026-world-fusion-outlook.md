On November 12, the International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA) released its World Fusion Outlook 2026 during the third meeting of the World Fusion Energy Group, held at IAEA headquarters in Vienna, Austria.
The report highlights record private investment, expanding international cooperation, and growing efforts to establish the regulatory and supply chain infrastructure necessary for fusion commercialization. Drawing on data from the Fusion Industry Association’s 2026 Global Fusion Industry report, the publication notes that private fusion companies raised a record $4.5 billion in the 12 months leading to July 2026, bringing cumulative funding since 2021 to more than $14 billion.
The report also examines the financing requirements, regulatory considerations, and industrial partnerships needed to move fusion technologies toward commercial deployment.
FIA CEO Andrew Holland participated in the meeting, and contributed to the publication’s drafting and review.
