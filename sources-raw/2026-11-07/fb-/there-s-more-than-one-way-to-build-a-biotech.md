As biotech companies continue to navigate a challenging funding environment, Axiom Biosciences is taking an unconventional approach to raising capital and developing new therapies.
The company is pursuing a public listing in Hong Kong, with ambitions to eventually list on the Nasdaq. Meanwhile, it's developing several drug delivery platforms, including a stem cell therapy approach that doesn't rely on genetic modification.
In this episode of "The Top Line," Fierce Biotech's Marissa Russo speaks with Axiom founder and CEO Remo Moomiaie-Qajar about the company's financing strategy, its approach to drug development and what's next for its clinical pipeline.
