Novartis cites more tumor reports after Zolgensma
Novartis has disclosed seven previously unreported cases of tumors in children who received Zolgensma, its gene therapy for spinal muscular atrophy. The company has received nine tumor reports in all since the treatment was approved in 2019, STAT’s Ed Silverman and Jason Mast write.
