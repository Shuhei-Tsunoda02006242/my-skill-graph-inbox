GE HealthCare has launched ViewPoint Ultra, an ultrasound workflow solution designed to help health systems standardize clinical documentation and reporting across sites and departments while supporting specialized individual care needs.
Ultrasound is increasingly being used across an expanding range of healthcare departments and locations.
A 2025 national survey of U.S. Veterans Affairs medical centers found that point of care ultrasound use was detected across its facilities in aiding critical care, hospital medicine, general surgery, anesthesiology and emergency medicine.
But in a national survey of U.S. emergency departments, just over half reported having a point-of-care ultrasound workflow that includes both image storage and physician review.
Each healthcare facility has its own unique workflows and documentation requirements, and managing these processes across multiple systems can add complexity for clinical and IT teams.
Often, that means fragmented systems, inconsistent documentation, duplicated IT effort, and growing governance requirements.
ViewPoint Ultra is designed to bring ultrasound workflows together in a single, scalable solution to support women’s health, cardiovascular ultrasound, point-of-care ultrasound, and radiology.
“Clinicians need tools that fit the way they work, and health systems need solutions that can grow with them,” said Ryan Dobbe, General Manager of Advanced Imaging Solutions Digital at GE HealthCare, in a statement.
“ViewPoint Ultra brings those needs together, helping teams simplify ultrasound documentation and reporting while supporting the specialized workflows they rely on every day and giving them tools to work effectively.”
The platform features specialized workflows across care areas, simplified documentation and reporting tools, and web-based access to enable authorized clinicians to review and finalize documentation across locations and remotely.
