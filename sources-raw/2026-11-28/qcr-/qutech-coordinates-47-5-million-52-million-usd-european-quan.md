QuTech is coordinating the next 3.5-year phase of the Quantum Internet Alliance (QIA), a consortium of more than 50 universities, telecom operators, and quantum technology companies across 12 European member states backed by €47.5 million ($52 million USD) in funding from the European Commission. Led by QIA Director and QuTech Professor Stephanie Wehner, the program transitions from component R&D carried out between 2022 and March 2026 to integrating quantum repeaters, integrated photonics, control electronics, and network software into a programmable full-stack quantum internet prototype.
The consortium aims to connect two metropolitan-scale quantum networks over a long-distance optical fiber link capable of running distributed quantum applications and linking quantum processors, while establishing open-access pilot facilities alongside Dutch ecosystem partners including Delft Networks, Falqon Systems, and Qblox.
