Four researchers at QuTech and Delft University of Technology, Menno Veldhorst, Lieven Vandersypen, Giordano Scappucci, and Fabio Sebastiano, have been awarded a six-year European Research Council (ERC) Synergy Grant for a project titled UniQ (Unified Semiconductor Quantum Computer) aimed at scaling semiconductor spin-qubit processors. Selected as one of 48 funded proposals out of 957 submissions in the 2026 ERC Synergy call, the initiative brings together materials engineering, qubit physics, cryogenic electronics, and multi-qubit integration to overcome the wiring and control bottlenecks of large spin-qubit arrays.
Rather than routing separate control and readout lines from room temperature to every individual qubit, UniQ will develop low-noise germanium heterostructures capable of hosting both uniform spin qubits and on-chip cryogenic CMOS pre-amplifiers and signal-routing circuits. The project will also combine simplified voltage-pulse qubit control with mobile spin-shuttling arrays to increase qubit connectivity and support lower-overhead quantum error-correcting codes.
