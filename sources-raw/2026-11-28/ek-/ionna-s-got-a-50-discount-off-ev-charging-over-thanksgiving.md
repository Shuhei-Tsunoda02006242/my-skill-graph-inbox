IONNA has a great deal for Thanksgiving EV road-trippers: Charging across its entire fast-charging network is half off until thep=[]””” end of Sunday, November 29.
That’s just $0.20 per kWh, plus tax, which is pretty awesome. IONNA’s regular network-wide rate is capped at $0.39 per kWh and requires no paid membership.
Here’s what that looks like in actual dollars: Adding 50 kWh ordinarily costs up to $19.50 before tax at an IONNA charger. During the sale, it’ll cost $10 – a savings of $9.50.
Any compatible EV can charge for $0.20 per kWh over the holiday weekend, but automakers that founded IONNA have also rolled out additional discounts that will stack with the IONNA Thanksgiving weekend discount. Drivers of those cars just need to start their session using their automaker’s app or Plug & Charge.
Its Rechargeries offer both NACS and CCS connectors and charging speeds of up to 400 kW.
IONNA’s Rechargery locator shows locations, live availability, connector types, and amenities, as well as which makes and models get extra discounts with Plug & Charge.
