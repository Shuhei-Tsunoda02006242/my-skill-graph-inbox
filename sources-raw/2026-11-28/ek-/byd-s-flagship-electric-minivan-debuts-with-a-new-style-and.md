BYD’s new flagship MPV is not your average minivan. The Whale can drive over 559 miles (900 km) on a single charge, charge in as little as five minutes, and it’s packed with BYD’s latest tech.
Meet the BYD Whale, a new flagship electric minivan
The Whale is the first flagship multi-purpose vehicle (MPV) from BYD’s Ocean series. BYD has several sub-brands, including Denza, Yangwang, and Fang Cheng Bao, and sells vehicles under two primary lineups: Ocean and Dynasty.
BYD unveiled the Whale for the first time at the Guangzhou Auto Show on November 27, with a launch set for the first quarter of 2027.
Measuring 5,270 mm long, 1,996 mm wide, and 1,815 mm tall, with a 3,130 mm wheelbase, BYD’s newest minivan is slightly longer than the Chrysler Pacifica.
The Whale will be available in both plug-in hybrid (PHEV) and pure electric (EV) powertrains. Both will use BYD’s latest Blade Batteries 2.0. The PHEV version will offer a pure-electric CLTC range of 248 miles (400 km), while the EV variant offers over 559 miles (900 km) of range on a single charge.
With BYD’s Flash Charging tech, the electric minivan can charge from 10% to 70% in just five minutes.
For a full recharge, or to go from 10% to 97%, it takes only nine minutes. Even in temperatures as low as -30°C(-22°F), it takes just 3 minutes longer, or 12 minutes total.
Leading up to its debut, camouflaged prototypes were spotted with a boxy design that looked closer to a traditional gas-powered MPV.
However, the Whale looks better than expected. It adopts BYD’s updated Ocean design with smooth edges and a double-layer staggered grille.

BYD didn’t reveal many details outside of confirming the name and showcasing the design. With LiDAR on the roof, the Whale will offer BYD’s latest Advanced Driver Assistance System (ADAS).
The Whale will go on sale in the first quarter of 2027, joining the Dynasty Series Xia, Linghui M9, and Denza D9 as BYD’s fourth MPV.
Prices will be revealed closer to launch, but the Whale is expected to start at around 300,000 yuan ($45,000), filling the gap between the Xia, which starts at 249,800 yuan ($37,500), and the Denza D9 at 359,800 yuan ($54,000).
