I was three days into calling doctors’ offices, trying to get a primary care appointment. Over and over, I sat on hold, only to hear I’d have to wait weeks or months. It was mind numbing.
Then I reached a voicemail greeting that sounded about as despondent as I felt.
“We’ve received an extremely high volume of messages due to multiple providers in our area retiring or leaving their practice very recently,” the woman’s voice said with a hint of exasperation, “so please understand it may take longer than normal to receive a return call back.”
