Insider Brief
- Irish quantum computing company Equal1 advanced to due diligence for a potential equity investment of up to €30 million from the European Innovation Council Fund.
- Equal1 would use the funding to expand production and deployment of its on-premises quantum computers.
- The University College Dublin spinout uses standard CMOS manufacturing processes to integrate silicon spin qubits, control electronics and error correction on a single chip.
PRESS RELEASE — Equal1 today announced it has been selected to advance to the due-diligence phase with the European Innovation Council (EIC) Fund under the Strategic Technologies for Europe Platform (STEP) Scale Up program. The selection opens the path for a potential equity investment of up to €30 million.
The prospective EIC investment will accelerate Equal1’s commercialization roadmap, scale the production and deployment of its on-premise quantum computing solutions, and strengthen Europe’s technological sovereignty in quantum computing.
Equal1, a University College Dublin (UCD) spinout, is pioneering the integration of quantum silicon spin qubits, control electronics and error correction integrated in CMOS silicon and fabricated using standard CMOS semiconductor technology. This approach eliminates the massive infrastructure barriers associated with legacy quantum systems, advancing the company’s vision to make quantum computing as commonplace and transformative as GPUs.
Jason Lynch, CEO of Equal1, said: “Advancing to the due-diligence phase of the EIC STEP Scale Up program is a tremendous milestone for Equal1 and a strong endorsement of our silicon-based quantum architecture and our team. By building quantum processors using standard CMOS semiconductor processes, we are making scalable, on-premise quantum computing a practical reality. This prospective support from the EIC Fund-alongside our private co-investors and the ongoing backing of Enterprise Ireland-will enable us to accelerate our commercialization roadmap and position Europe at the forefront of the global quantum computing industry.”
The EIC STEP Scale Up program provides equity investments of €10–30 million to help European deep tech companies scale strategic technologies. Deployed alongside private co-investors, these investments are designed to catalyze larger funding rounds and bolster Europe’s long-term competitiveness and technological sovereignty in critical fields, including quantum computing, AI, energy, and advanced materials.
Minister for Enterprise, Tourism and Employment, Peter Burke TD, said: “I would like to warmly congratulate Jason and the entire team at Equal1 on being selected to advance in the EIC STEP Scale Up program. This achievement is a testament to the world-class deep tech innovation emerging from Ireland. Quantum computing represents a transformational frontier for global industry, and Equal1’s progress underscores Ireland’s vital role in driving European technological leadership and competitiveness.”
Jenny Melia, CEO of Enterprise Ireland, said: “Equal1’s selection for the EIC STEP Scale Up due-diligence phase is a phenomenal win for the company and for Ireland’s deep tech ecosystem. Enterprise Ireland is proud to support ambitious Irish innovators as they compete and succeed at the highest levels of European funding through Horizon Europe and the EIC. Equal1’s breakthrough single-chip quantum technology has the potential to redefine high-performance computing globally, and we look forward to supporting the company as it scales internationally.”
