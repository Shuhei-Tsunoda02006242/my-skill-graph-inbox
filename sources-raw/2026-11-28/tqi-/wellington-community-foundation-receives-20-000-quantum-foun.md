Insider Brief
- The Wellington Community Foundation (WCF) received a $20,000 Quantum in the Community grant from the Quantum Foundation to support its community programmes.
- The grant will support WCF’s efforts to address the needs of vulnerable seniors, children and veterans in Palm Beach County.
- The foundation was recognised at a November 17 community breakfast attended by nonprofit leaders, community advocates and philanthropic partners.
The Wellington Community Foundation (WCF) received a $20,000 grant at the sixth annual Quantum in the Community Breakfast on Tuesday, Nov. 17, the Town-Crier reported. The foundation was one of the nonprofit organizations recognized at the event.
Nonprofit leaders, community advocates and philanthropic partners attended the breakfast. According to the source, the gathering recognized organizations and individuals who make a difference across Palm Beach County.
The outlet described the grant as an investment in WCF and its ongoing efforts to address the needs of the community’s most vulnerable seniors, children and veterans.
“The Wellington Community Foundation extends its sincere appreciation to Quantum Foundation for the $20,000 award and for its continued commitment to strengthening local communities,” WCF Chair Jim Sackett said.
At the breakfast, 84-year-old Marge “Mama” Jacobs, a Firebush Nonprofit representative and longtime community volunteer, received the Marie Thorpe Above & Beyond Award, as reported by the Town-Crier. The outlet said she was honored for her dedication to helping others and that she reflects the compassion, generosity and commitment to community service the award was created to recognize.
“It was a wonderful morning celebrating those who make a difference in our community. We are grateful to the Quantum Foundation for its generous support and continued partnership,” said Dawn Rivera, executive director of the Wellington Community Foundation.
Information about the foundation and ways to support its programs is available at www.wellingtoncommunityfoundation.org.
