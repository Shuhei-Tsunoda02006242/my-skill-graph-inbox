Welcome to this week's Chutes & Ladders, our roundup of significant leadership hirings, firings and retirings across the industry. Please send the good word—or the bad—from your shop to Darren Incorvaia, Marissa Russo or Donavyn Coffey, and it will be featured here at the end of each week.
B+L’s Brent Saunders eyes the exit again
Bausch + Lomb
Bausch + Lomb CEO Brent Saunders made a surprising second exit from the eye health company this week, less than four years into his latest tenure. Saunder’s exit was mutually agreed upon by the executive and the board of directors, Bausch + Lomb said in a statement.
In his stead, Thomas Appio, CEO of parent company Bausch Health since 2022 and Bosch + Lomb chairperson as of August, will lead as interim CEO.
“When I returned to Bausch + Lomb, I believed this company had tremendous potential,” said Saunders in a statement. “Today, it is stronger, more focused and better positioned for the future, with a clearer strategy, a more robust pipeline and significant opportunities ahead.”
Evercore ISI analysts noted that the change in leadership is unexpected given the pipeline projections and commercial targets Bausch + Lomb had laid out. Appio’s interim takeover suggests the transition may have been a surprise to the company as well, the analyst speculated. Story
Paragon imported Apogee's top brass after AbbVie buyout
Paragon Labs
Four months after AbbVie announced its $10.9 billion deal to buy Apogee for its late-phase eczema drug zumilokibart, it appears Paragon has taken its pick of the biotech’s top talent. This includes Michael Henderson, M.D., who headed up Apogee until the sale and will now take up the same role at Paragon. Henderson will be joined at Paragon by several of his Apogee colleagues, namely head of R&D Carl Dambkowski, M.D., chief business and financial officer Dan Mulreany, chief legal officer Matt Batters and chief people officer Emily Cox.
The Apogee alumni are no strangers to Paragon, as zumilokibart was originally developed at Paragon before the Boston-based biotech drug discovery hub spun out Apogee to take the anti-IL-13 antibody forward. Story
RAS-focused Verastem unveils leadership transition
Verastem Oncology
Effective in the new year, Daniel Paterson will step down as CEO after a 15-year run at Verastem, with Michael Bailey set to take over as the cancer drugmaker's next helmsman. Paterson was involved in the acquisition of VS-7375 from GenFleet Therapeutics, as well as its rapid advancement to phase 2 clinical trials across multiple KRAS G12D-mutated tumor types. VS-7375 is an oral KRAS G12D dual ON/OFF inhibitor that just recently reported positive phase 1/2 data in multiple solid tumors, including metastatic pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma.
Bailey has served on Verastem’s board since May 2026, but prior to that had spent 16 years with Aveo Pharmaceuticals in multiple leadership positions, including chief business officer, chief commercial officer, and most recently CEO. Release
