Insider Brief
- The Quantum Insider launched analyst-verified quantum market datasets sold individually as Excel workbooks and PDF briefings.
- The three initial datasets cover quantum computer sales, government funding and market forecasts through 2035.
- Each dataset costs $2,999 for a single license or $5,999 for an organization-wide corporate license.
The Quantum Insider (“TQI”), the market intelligence platform for quantum technology, today launched Datasets: analyst-verified quantum market information, sold one dataset at a time as an Excel workbook and a PDF briefing. Three are available at launch and four more are scheduled.
Every record in a dataset has been checked by a TQI analyst against the document it came from. The Resonance Intelligence Engine monitors tens of thousands of sources and structures candidate records. Quantum-specialist analysts then verify each material record against its primary source and classify it against the TQI quantum taxonomy, in use since 2019.
The TQI Intelligence Platform is still the whole database: queryable, updated daily, with TQI market reports included. A dataset is one table out of it, packaged so a buyer can own the specific cut they need for a one-off use case.
The Quantum Computing Sales Dataset records 204 publicly disclosed quantum computer sales, leases, across 55 vendors and 26 countries, with a catalog of 75 priced systems. The Global Government Quantum Funding Dataset holds 657 program lines for 63 nations and the European Union, each with its agency, budget stage, and a source. The Quantum Market Sizing Dataset is the model behind TQI’s published forecasts: 2025 to 2035 by segment, geography, and vertical, under bear, base, and bull scenarios, with every assumption exposed so the buyer can change it and rerun the model.
Four further datasets are scheduled: US state quantum programs, quantum networking and sensing deployments, quantum patents, and quantum investors. TQI will set the order of what follows by what buyers ask for.
Each dataset is $2,999 for a single license, or $5,999 for a corporate license that covers everyone at the buyer’s organization, including use in client deliverables. Datasets, and a free hub of guides built on them, are at https://thequantuminsider.com/datasets/.
