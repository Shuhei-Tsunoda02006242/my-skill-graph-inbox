Longtime Takeda R&D chief to depart next June
Takeda
      
      
Takeda President of Research and Development Andy Plump, M.D., Ph.D., is leaving the company in June after twelve years in the position. Plump joined the Japanese Big Pharma in 2015 from Sanofi, where he was chief medical and scientific officer. The executive will continue to lead R&D for Takeda until a successor is named and will help with the transition through the middle of next year.
The announcement comes as Takeda looks to grow revenue at a time when its exclusivity on ADHD drug Vyvanse has dragged on sales. Last month, the FDA approved narcolepsy treatment Orzeyful and blood cancer drug Mimrylo. In the last five years, the company has won approval for the antiviral Livtencity, blood clotting disorder drug Adzynma and eosinophilic esophagitis therapy Eohilia.
A trained internal medicine physician with a fellowship in medical genetics, Plump was also worldwide cardiovascular research head at Merck & Co., where he oversaw cardiovascular research, preclinical development and translational sciences. Story
      
Sofinnova lands Gilead alum as executive partner
Sofinnova Investments
Biopharmaceutical investment firm Sofinnova announced the addition of Merdad Parsey, M.D., Ph.D., to the investment team as an executive partner. Parsey hails from his executive vice president and chief medical officer role at Gilead. Prior to Gilead, he held several tenure positions at other Big Pharmas such as Merck & Co., Regeneron and Genentech.
“I have had the opportunity to work across early research and clinical development through regulatory approval and commercialization, with the goal of bringing innovative, important new medicines to patients,” Parsey said in the release. “I look forward to being part of the Sofinnova team to continue those efforts.”
Parsey joins an already stacked Sofinnova partners team that includes Sanofi and Biogen alum Maha Radhakrishnan, M.D., Roche, Amgen and Merck leader Jonathan Leff, M.D., and Merck and GSK alum Chris Carpenter, M.D., Ph.D., among others. Since its founding, Sofinnova has built companies in the U.S. and Europe with approximately $4.8 billion in assets under management and committed capital, as of the end of June. Release
Pyxis locks in interim CEO in preparation for phase 1 results
Pyxis Oncology
Ahead of a Phase 1 readout for its first-in-class antibody drug conjugate (ADC), Pyxis Oncology has appointed its interim CEO, Thomas Civik, to a permanent appointment as CEO and chairman of the board.
Civik stepped up as interim CEO in February of 2026 and has been a member of Pyxis’ board of directors since 2021. Prior to Pyxis, Civik was chairman of ImCheck Therapeutics which sold to Ispen and Repare Therapeutics which was in turn sold to Xoma. He was also president and CEO of Five Prime Therapeutics until it was acquired by Amgen.
With Civik at the helm, Pyxis co-founder and founding chairman, John Flavin, will transition to serve as lead independent director of the board. Pyxis expects to report data from its MICVO Phase 1 study in second-line and beyond recurrent/metastatic head and neck squamous cell carcinoma this fall. Release
