Legend poaches Novartis vet to boost commercial potential
Legend Biotech
      
      
After former Legend Biotech CEO Ying Huang, Ph.D., left the company suddenly in July, Legend has found its permanent CEO, poaching Novartis veteran Ingrid Zhang as the CAR-T specialist eyes its next phase of growth.
Zhang assumed the post from interim chief Alan Bash, who has returned to his role as president of Carvykti. Zhang joined Legend after a 15-year tenure at Novartis, most recently serving as chief commercial officer for Novartis’ ex-U.S. international business. Before that, she held positions at AstraZeneca, Pfizer and McKinsey.
The Big Pharma vet will focus on maximizing Carvykti’s commercial potential and meeting full market demand as new competitors emerge. In the second quarter of 2026, Johnson & Johnson-partnered Carvykti generated $657 million in sales, driven by 32% year-over-year growth in the U.S. and 128% expansion ex-U.S. as the multiple myeloma drug reached 19 global markets. Serapha revamps C-suite with Regenxbio founder
Serapha Bio
After launching through a merger and $230 million financing, Serapha Bio has loaded up its C-suite with several new appointees. The genetic medicines company has named Kenneth Mills CEO and added Daphne Karydas as president and CFO. Weston Miller, M.D., will be its new chief medical officer, Nina Hunter, Ph.D., was named chief development officer and Shiva Fritsch rounds out the new leadership as chief corporate affairs officer.
With investment led by RA Capital Management and RTW Investments, Serapha is supporting SERP-01, an in vivo base editing program developed in China and licensed from YolTech Therapeutics. The asset, which is being investigated as a potential treatment for alpha-1-antitrypsin deficiency, targets the SERPINA1 E342K mutation.
Mills most recently served as president and CEO of Tagworks Pharmaceuticals and spent 15 years as president and CEO of Regenxbio after founding the company. He led its 2015 IPO and built it into a gene therapy company with research, clinical and manufacturing capabilities. Release
Chiesi taps Flagship Pioneering alum to lead innovation
Chiesi Group
Specialty and rare disease pharma Chiesi is set to welcome David Khougazian as its next Group CEO. Khougazian joins from biotech venture capital firm Flagship Pioneering, where he was the executive partner of growth and head of global engagement. Khougazian’s appointment marks a shift toward innovation-driven growth for the Italian pharma, marked by recent acquisitions, licensing deals and increased R&D spending.
“[David] brings a unique combination of global healthcare leadership, operational experience and exposure to some of the most dynamic areas of biotech innovation, which makes him exceptionally well positioned to guide Chiesi through this next chapter,” said Maria Paola Chiesi, Chiesi Group Chair.
For his part, Khougazian said he’s looking forward to working with the Chiesi team to “further strengthen the Group's innovation capabilities, advance its pipeline and partnerships.” Khougazian will succeed interim Group CEO Jean-Marc Bellemin on October 15, 2026. Bellemin will continue in his permanent role as CFO. Release
