French neutral-atom quantum computing developer Pasqal (NASDAQ: PSQL) has signed a three-year Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with LG CNS, the AI transformation (AX) and IT services subsidiary of LG Group. Timed alongside the France-Korea Summit in Paris, the strategic partnership focuses on integrating Pasqal’s neutral-atom quantum processing units (QPUs) into LG CNS’s AI Haus environments and commercial AI Data Center infrastructure across South Korea and the wider Asia-Pacific (APAC) region.
The agreement establishes an operational roadmap to deploy hybrid quantum-classical computing platforms, pairing Pasqal’s neutral-atom hardware with LG CNS’s enterprise cloud management and system integration capabilities. The partnership targets high-performance, compute-intensive workloads across industrial, scientific, and enterprise AI domain applications, establishing operational frameworks for resource management and dynamic workload execution within hybrid data center architectures.

| • Go-to-Market Expansion | • APAC regional hub anchoring in South Korea | • Commercial deployment across domestic & global enterprise markets | 
Under CEO Wasiq Bokhari, the alliance deepens Pasqal’s commercial and institutional presence in South Korea, which serves as its primary hub for APAC operations. Building on strategic industrial ties and backing from Korean institutional investors—including LG Electronics and Dunamu & Partners—the initiative follows Pasqal’s business combination with Bleichroeder Acquisition Corp. II and its Nasdaq listing, expanding its hardware footprint across global enterprise AI infrastructure.
