Welcome to this week's Chutes & Ladders, our roundup of significant leadership hirings, firings and retirings across the industry. Please send the good word—or the bad—from your shop to Donavyn Coffey, Marissa Russo and Will Maddox, and it will be featured here at the end of each week.
Travere chief makes way for former Amicus CEO
Travere Therapeutics
      
      
Travere Therapeutics CEO Eric Dube, Ph.D., is stepping down after overseeing the company's launch of the rare kidney disease drug Filspari. On Dec. 1, Bradley Campbell will take the reins after leading Amicus Therapeutics through its recent $4.8 billion acquisition by BioMarin. Dube will stay on as an executive advisor through Feb. 15, 2027, to support the transition.
Travere said Dube is leaving to focus on his family. He rebranded the company as Travere to distance it from its original name, Retrophin, which was founded by “pharma bro” Martin Shkreli. In 2023, the company won FDA accelerated approval for its IgA nephropathy medicine Filspari.
Campbell arrives with more than 20 years of rare disease experience, joining Amicus in 2006 before succeeding founder and longtime helmsman John Crowley in 2022. During his tenure, Amicus graduated from a single-product company in 2023 after scoring FDA approval for its Pombiliti + Opfolda regimen for Pompe disease. Story
      
Ex-Cue CEO takes the reins at Lift
Lift Therapeutics
Lift Therapeutics has tapped former Cue Biopharma CEO Daniel Passeri as chief executive as it works to advance its lead immuno-modulatory alpha neutrophil (iMAN) program into human trials in 2027. The London-based biotech is developing a new class of immunotherapy for hard-to-treat cancers.
“I am honored to join at this pivotal stage and work closely with Bo [director and president of Lift] to lead the team as we advance our first iMAN therapeutic into the clinic in early 2027 while continuing to build a differentiated portfolio of transformational, allogeneic, neutrophil-based therapies designed to address some of the most challenging tumors,” Passeri said in a release.
Passeri joins Lift after a nine-year run as CEO of Cue Biopharma, which focuses on immunological disorders. Before that, he served as president and CEO of oncology biotech Curis. Release
Nuclera’s new leader sets sights on growth
Nuclera
Protein science biotech Nuclera has brought Carl Raimond on as CEO as the company looks to scale. Co-founder and former CEO Michael Chen, Ph.D., will move into the chief scientific and business officer post.
Raimond previously served as president of Olink Proteomics, which he led through its $3 billion acquisition by Thermo Fisher Scientific in 2023. He also held senior leadership posts at Agilent Technologies and Affymetrix.
“Now, we’ve reached an important point in the company’s evolution and Carl is the right CEO to lead us forward,” Chen said in a statement. “Carl’s experience building and scaling life-science businesses, combined with his understanding of the opportunity ahead, will be a huge asset to Nuclera.”
      
Raimond said he aims to sharpen the company’s focus on customer adoption, commercial execution and expanding applications of its technology. The Nuclera leadership transition is effective immediately. Release
