Financial Overview & Post-Listing Capital
Pasqal generated €4.87 million in revenue for H1 2026, representing a 13.7% increase year-over-year compared to €4.29 million in H1 2025. Top-line growth was primarily anchored by a 34% increase in QPU-related services revenue, which rose to €3.9 million.
The company reported €70.4 million in booked and awarded business as of June 30, 2026, combining multi-year customer contracts, government grants, and tax credits.
Operating loss for H1 2026 expanded to (€59.16) million, up from (€19.77) million in H1 2025. The widening operating loss included €27.3 million in share-based payment charges (compared to €1.0 million in H1 2025) and €10.2 million in one-time transaction-related expenses incurred for its public listing. Net loss for the period stood at (€53.24) million.
As of June 30, 2026, cash and cash equivalents totaled €110.8 million. Following the successful completion of its SPAC merger and related financing on August 27, 2026, Pasqal’s balance sheet was significantly strengthened to approximately €312.9 million ($355.8M) in cash and cash equivalents, providing substantial runway for ongoing technology execution.
Hardware & Technological Breakthroughs
- 1,024-Atom Scaling: Prepared defect-free registers of 1,024 physical atoms, doubling its previous 506-atom milestone. A redesigned cryogenic platform extended atom lifetimes by approximately 40-fold.
- Photonic Integrated Circuit (PIC) Chip Trapping: Trapped four rubidium atoms using laser light generated directly by a Photonic Integrated Circuit (PIC) co-developed with subsidiary Aeponyx. The platform achieved 27.5-second lifetimes while reducing the optical footprint by up to 50x, addressing a key scaling constraint for neutral-atom hardware.
- Logical Qubit Execution: Demonstrated a full machine-learning application for solving differential equations using logical qubits, showing that the logical implementation outperformed its physical-qubit counterpart on a full end-to-end workload.
- NVIDIA CUDA-Q Integration: Integrated NVIDIA CUDA-Q into the Pasqal platform to simplify hybrid quantum-classical workflows across high-performance computing (HPC) centers.
Commercial Deployments & Strategic Expansions
- Middle East Expansion: Launched Saudi Arabia’s first quantum computer—a 200-qubit system offering Quantum Computing as a Service (QCaaS). Signed MOUs with Eleven Ventures and KACST to commercialize quantum computing and advance R&D across Saudi Arabia.
- European On-Premises Installations: Deployed Italy’s first neutral-atom QPU (140 qubits) for integration with the Leonardo supercomputer. Advanced capital markets workflows in partnership with Crédit Agricole CIB.
- Canadian Photonic Supply Chain: Established a Center of Competency in PIC Packaging through Aeponyx at the C2MI facility in Bromont, Quebec.
- XPRIZE Finalist: Selected as one of five global finalists in the three-year XPRIZE Quantum Applications competition sponsored by Google Quantum AI and GESDA.
