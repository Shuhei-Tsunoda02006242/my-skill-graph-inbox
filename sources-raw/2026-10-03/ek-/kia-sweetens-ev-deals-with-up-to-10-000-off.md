With the new EV3 now arriving at dealerships, Kia is throwing some extra savings into the mix with up to $10,000 off in customer cash on select EVs.
Kia EV deals and discounts in October
The 2027 EV3 went on sale in mid-August, starting at about $30,000 as Kia’s most affordable electric vehicle.
In its first full month of sales, Kia sold just over 500 EV3s. After another record quarter in the US, Kia aims to carry the momentum into the end of the year and achieve its fourth consecutive record sales year.
Kia announced on Thursday that the new 2027 EV3 has officially arrived at dealerships across the US.
The EV3 offers many of the advanced features found in the EV9, in a smaller, more affordable package. It’s available in five trims: Light, Wind, Land, GT-Line, and a range-topping GT model. The cheapest EV3 Light uses a 58.3 kWh battery that provides up to 221 miles of range.
Higher-priced trims, including the Wind, Land, GT-Line, and GT, use an 81.4 kWh battery for up to 321 miles of range.
All EV3 models come with a native NACS port, so you can use Tesla Superchargers without a separate adapter.
Charging from 10% to 80% takes about 29 minutes with the 58.3 kWh battery. The 81.4 kWh battery takes around 31 minutes.
Inside, all EV3 models feature Kia’s latest ccNC (connected car Navigation Cockpit) with standard wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto. The setup offers close to 30″ of screen space combined, including a dual 12.3″ instrument cluster and touchscreen infotainment, plus a 5″ climate control display.
How much does it cost to lease? Kia is offering a special lease rate for the EV3 Wind FWD, starting at $349/month.
The offer is for the EV3 Wind FWD model with $3,999 due at signing for 24 months. Or, you can opt for a 36-month lease for $359/month, with $3,999 due at signing.
Looking for AWD? The 2027 EV3 Wind AWD also has a special lease offer of $399 for 24 months with $3,999 due at signing. Or, again, a 36-month lease is available for $409/month. That still requires $3,999 due at signing.

On the other hand, Kia is offering a $1,500 customer cash discount on all EV3 models, or 0,90% APR for 48 months.
The $30,000 EV3 Light currently has no special offer. Using Kia’s payment calculator, it would be about $352/month to lease for 24 months, with $3,999 due at signing. A 36-month lease would be about $350/month.
Looking for something a bit bigger? Kia is offering up to $10,000 in Customer Cash on the three-row EV9 this month.
Leases for the 2026 EV9 Light Long Range RWD are listed at $399 a month for 36 or 24 months, with $4,999 due at signing. Kia is also offering 0% APR, plus $5,000 APR Bonus Cash on 2026 EV9 models.
While the all-electric Niro is on its way out, it’s also available with a $10,000 Customer Cash discount while supply lasts. Kia is offering leases as low as $289/month for 36 months, with $3,999 due at signing.
The 2026 Kia EV6 is available with a $5,000 Customer Cash discount. Leases start at $369/month for 36 months, with $4,999 due at signing.
