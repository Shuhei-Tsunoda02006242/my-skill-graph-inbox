Insider Brief
- Symmatrics has appointed Andrew Hallman, a former senior CIA and ODNI official who now works at Leidos, to its Strategic Advisory Board.
- Hallman brings more than 30 years of intelligence and security experience, including serving as Deputy Director of the CIA for Digital Innovation and performing the duties of Principal Deputy Director of National Intelligence.
- Symmatrics is developing cybersecurity products based on One-Time Pad Symmetric Key (OTPSK) encryption, which the company says are designed to protect data and communications against AI-driven and quantum-related threats.
PRESS RELEASE — Symmatrics, the cybersecurity company pioneering one-time pad encryption at internet scale and building the new trust layer for the AI and post-quantum era, today announced the appointment of Andrew Hallman to its Strategic Advisory Board. Hallman joins the company as organizations across government, defense, and critical infrastructure sectors face escalating cybersecurity risks driven by artificial intelligence and the emergence of quantum computing.
A respected national security leader, Hallman brings more than three decades of intelligence and security experience, including senior leadership roles at both the Central Intelligence Agency (CIA) and the Office of the Director of National Intelligence (ODNI). He is currently Vice President and Strategic Account Executive for the Intelligence Community at Leidos, Inc.
“Andrew’s experience leading the nation’s most critical intelligence and security initiatives makes him a valuable addition to our advisory board,” said Walter Raquet, Chairman and CEO of Symmatrics. “As AI accelerates cyber threats and quantum computing challenges traditional encryption, Andrew’s perspective will help guide our efforts to develop technologies protecting governments, enterprises, and critical infrastructure from the next generation of cyber risks.”
From 2019-2020, Hallman served as Principal Executive, Office of the Director of National Intelligence, performing the duties of Principal Deputy Director of National Intelligence, where he helped oversee the U.S. Intelligence Community and served as a principal adviser to the President on intelligence issues. From 2015-2019, Hallman stood up and led one of the Central Intelligence Agency’s newest directorates, serving as the Deputy Director of CIA for Digital Innovation.
“Symmatrics‘ breakthrough in one-time pad encryption at scale uniquely positions the company to address the most significant security challenges of our time,” said Hallman. “Symmatrics approach prevents attacks by eliminating much of the attack surfaces targeted by AI and quantum and better protects sensitive networks and communications with device and user identity management. I look forward to supporting the team in safeguarding our country’s organizations.”
Symmatrics is pioneering a new model for cybersecurity built around One-Time Pad Symmetric Key (OTPSK) encryption, delivering AI resistant and quantum secure protection designed to safeguard data in motion and at rest. Leveraging patented technologies and Continuously Expiring Trust™, the company is helping organizations prepare for today’s AI-driven attacks and tomorrow’s post-quantum security challenges. The company serves organizations across national security, defense, financial services, healthcare, critical infrastructure, and other high-risk industries.
